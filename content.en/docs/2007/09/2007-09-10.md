## Monday, 10 September 2007

## Schwarzenbronn – Dollnstein

Just before Eichstätt I stop at the _Taube_ inn. Anything further would be too much.

Since Rothenburg I've been freezing and thoroughly chilled. The ride was still very impressive.

Above all, the last quarter through the narrow Altmühl valley was glorious. Before that: monotonous pedalling across plains that seemed to go on for ever.

A mental and physical ordeal, made almost unbearable by the constantly whistling (head)wind.

Still, I made it and I'm completely content. And tired. And looking forward to tomorrow.

Oh, and: today I got properly wet for the first time. Right now it looks as if it's settling in to rain.

We'll see. Tomorrow is supposed to be nice(r). I don't care what the weather does anyway…

**Stats:** 145km / approx. 10 hours
