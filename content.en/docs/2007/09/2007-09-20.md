## Thursday, 20 September 2007

## Budapest – Munich 😃 ☀

The night train was exhausting and I'm wondering whether it was the right decision.

Now I'm lying in bed in Munich. It's midday, I feel scattered and pointless.

I'm starving. Was it a good trip? Yes.

I'm already pining for these days. Already. The silence and freedom I got to experience were indescribably beautiful. Very intense moments alternated with dull, torture-like stretches.

Madness! I'm glad everything worked out so well, I'm noting down what I could do better next time, and I'm looking forward to it – to the next adventure.

And there's a medium-sized tear of farewell, too.
