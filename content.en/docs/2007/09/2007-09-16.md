Off to Budapest!

## Leaving Vienna – Lipót – every mood going ☀ 😎

The goodbye was short and 'bearable'. No tears, just looking forward to next weekend in Pattensen, near Hanover, Germany.

From the Westbahnhof I raced once more through the whole city in the morning – in perfect weather.

Along the Prater, through lush green. And then there was Bratislava.

My backside hurts like hell and pedalling was really hard (headwind!).

Great effort. Lovely weather, which I wanted to make the most of until the evening. I enjoy my (one-sided) sunburn and drift off blissfully.

Looking forward to Győr and the thermal baths in Komárom.
