## Monday, 17 September 2007

## Lipót – Komárom 😃 ☀ Brilliant blue sky

The day starts with my first Hungarian breakfast at the "White Swan", which is rather meagre. To my horror I notice that my energy bars are running low.

The route is hard. Flat, but technically very demanding because of the gravel track.

![](/images/2007/09/17/1443449607.jpg)

The bike often slides out from under me, but I've got it well under control. A long stretch takes me through Slovakia (SK), which doesn't really do it for me. It's strange there, colder than in Hungary.

And I'm glad to celebrate my debut as a proper camping cyclist at a campsite back in Hungary.

Shortly after the tent is up, it starts to rain. Soon after, I swim a few lengths in the thermal baths and enjoy feeling weightless in the water.

I sleep restlessly and dream of Riki suffering terrible bike crashes.

**Stats:** 120km / approx. 10 hours
