## Sunday, 9 September 2007

## Edith's birthday

Well rested, off I go again.

![](/images/2007/09/09/4616272589.jpg)

I ride to just before Rothenburg. The route is lovely. Bad Mergentheim doesn't want to let me go at first.

I pedal around aimlessly and finally find the route. Hooray!

The route is properly hilly and punchy climbs await my tired legs. I keep wishing for a taxi.

The hump up to the inn, where the celebrations are being held, finishes me off.

Still, I make it and reward myself with a short stretch and a hearty _Vesper_ in Edith's company.

![](/images/2007/09/09/4616888694.jpg)

First night under a stranger's roof.

approx. 60km / 5 hours :)
