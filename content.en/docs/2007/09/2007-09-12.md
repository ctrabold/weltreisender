## Wednesday, 12 September 2007

## Regensburg – Passau

Today is a rest day and I take the train to Passau.

I can still feel yesterday and I'm pretty muddled. Looking forward to putting my feet up later.

As it turns out, that's not happening.

From Passau, probably the most boring stretch of my tour awaits. Kilometre after kilometre alongside the main road, with the scent of exhaust fumes. Country road on the left, Danube on the right. Nothing else. On an endlessly straight stretch.

Then a switch to the road. The cycle path now runs along a quiet country road. Then suddenly: the wildest forest track. And a no-cycling sign, which I ignore after a moment's thought.

What follows is the most exciting part, over rocks and roots. I ride a demanding mountain-bike trail through mud and mire, decorated with big rocks and dense undergrowth. Suddenly the path ends.

A small gorge blocks my way. I unload the bike and carry my things over one by one. In the process I set a pannier rolling, and it only stops many metres further down the valley.

After an exhausting search I heave my things across the gorge. On we go. But not for long: the path turns out to be a dead end, which, sadly, I only realise, exhausted, almost an hour later.

Out of nowhere a Taiwanese man appears and helps me look for the right way.

Completely done in, I head back. A wise decision, because at this point on the Danube cycle path you _have_ to take a ferry across to the other bank. The detour turned out to be riskier than I'd thought. I'm hugely relieved to spend the evening safe and exhausted over a shandy and _Kaiserschmarrn_ (shredded sweet pancake) at the Gasthof _Stadt Wihnstadt_.
