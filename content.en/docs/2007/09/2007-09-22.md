## Saturday, 22 September 2007

## Munich – Dieburg 🏡

The Intercity train takes me home via Stuttgart to Darmstadt, and from there on to my hometown Dieburg.

![](/images/2007/09/22/1444314164.jpg)

### Looking back: where do I stand?

My first proper cycle tour is done and dusted. Almost two weeks on the road, 1050 kilometres in my legs, according to the bike computer at least.

In two weeks through Germany, Austria, a touch of Slovakia and Hungary. So many firsts! Amazing!

Two stages by train (from Regensburg to Passau, and from Linz to Vienna). Rest days were few: in Vienna I treat myself to two rest days to see more of the city. Totally worth it!

### What has stayed with me from the trip?

- **Cold and wet: You bet! I can do this!** Temperatures around 10 to 15 degrees, rain and headwind (ofcourse). It wears down my motivation. [I even wanted to give up]({{< ref "docs/2007/09/2007-09-11" >}}). I stuck with it and made it!
- **The dark gorge of hubris on the Danube:** due to lack of self-disciplin a pannier almost rolls into the river, the path ends in a dead end, pure stress – and still I keep a cool head. Then, out of nowhere, a completely relaxed [Taiwanese cyclist appears]({{< ref "docs/2007/09/2007-09-12" >}}). Priceless!
- **Hungarian Warmth at 25 degrees plus:** [my first night in the tent goes well]({{< ref "docs/2007/09/2007-09-17" >}}) and the warmth of the thermal baths gives me strength again. A lovely [Hungarian family rescues me from the dark]({{< ref "docs/2007/09/2007-09-18" >}}). Thank you!
- **1000 kilometres:** just before Budapest comes the big moment! [The magic 1000 km]({{< ref "docs/2007/09/2007-09-19" >}}) are reached, and the bike computer dies. Sadly, the magic happens without battery power but great spirit.
- **Vienna, simply beautiful:** Lipizzaners, [a siesta in front of the Butterfly House]({{< ref "docs/2007/09/2007-09-14" >}}), Belvedere and Schönbrunn. Well worth seeing!

I slept mostly in inns and guesthouses, once in the tent, and two times in private homes with families, and, well, a little on the night train.

### And now?

On 20 September 2007 I write in my diary:

> "I'm glad everything worked out so well, I'm noting down what I could do better next time, and I'm looking forward to it – to my next adventure.

And yes: with this "test ride" I wanted to explore whether I like cycle touring, what I should improve, what to ignore.

And yes, [the test was successful!]({{< ref "docs/preparation/before-departure" >}})

A good year and a half later, in May 2009, I cycle out of Dieburg again. With [the same tyres]({{< ref "docs/2009/05/2009-05-12" >}}), a new bike with a name and once more [on the beautiful Main cycle path]({{< ref "docs/2009/05/2009-05-21" >}}). This time the destination is: Singapore!
