## Wednesday, 19 September 2007

## Szödliget – Budapest 😃 ☀

After a delicious breakfast with Mamma Jambor and László, the bike computer ticks past 1000 kilometres.

![](/images/2007/09/19/4616949622.jpg)

Budapest lies at my feet. Or so I think. Sadly the route turns out to be very badly signposted, so the last stretch becomes a real adventure once more – or rather, gets on my nerves.

At some point the traffic picks up noticeably and I'm in Budapest. How romantic.

No town sign to pose for the camera… nothing.

![](/images/2007/09/19/1444336424.jpg)

Luckily the cycle path makes it easy to find my way through the city.

I see all the sights. The traffic is really annoying and spoils the fun a bit. After a long rest at Keleti, the East station, I head to the Gellért Baths to relax and get clean.

Before that, I bought a ticket to Munich. There's too little keeping me here, and I don't want to 'arrive' or stand still yet.

I spend the time until 8.30 pm splashing about and buying / eating food.

I even get to explore the city on a breakneck bus ride.

The city is very loud and hectic with all the traffic. At heart, though, it's lovely and beautiful.

**And: man, I've reached the goal of my journey!** Is this really my goal? That makes me sad. I'd like to keep riding. But reason says it's enough for now. My body is twinging a bit too, in my knee and back. Time to recover. Until next time.

Final count: 1050km
