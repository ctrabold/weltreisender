## Saturday, 8 September 2007

## A tough start

The saddle feels unfamiliar. The weather was middling to cold and windy.

Still, my nerves soon gave way to the joy of finally getting to turn the pedals.

The Main route was dull.

Lots of lawnmower-camping fanatics. Wertheim was further than I'd thought. The Tauber valley: lovely, really lovely.

Bad Mergentheim was stressful – with an empty head I kept taking wrong turns.

Arriving at Edith's is <3-warming.

And what a feeling, after a shower, to sit down to a _Vesper_ (a hearty Swabian cold supper) with Edith and Kurt and talk about nice things.

Thank you so much for your hospitality!

### Stats

170km / 9:30 hours :)
