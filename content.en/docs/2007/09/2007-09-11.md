## Tuesday, 11 September 2007

The ride to Regensburg lived up to its name: _Regen_ is German for rain.

It rained almost all the way. A strong, cold wind did the rest.

Completely shattered, I collapsed into a petrol station for a break and wanted to give up.

Go home. Home. Everywhere the icy wind reached in and tugged at my morale, at my strength. And yet – and precisely because of it – I made it.

I only paid Eichstätt and Kelheim a short visit. At times the Altmühl showed its best side, and now and then the sun came out. What a relief!

For kilometres I was on my own. Arriving in Regensburg was very emotional. A strange city: calm from the outside, stressful inside because of the traffic.

The room at the Hotel "Herzogshof" with a direct view of the cathedral (room no. 33) makes up for the hardships. The pasta was great too.
