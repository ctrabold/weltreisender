## Thursday, 13 September 2007

## Linz – Vienna ☀ 😕 > 😃

Today I'm meeting Riki in Vienna. My ray of light on this dreary route.

The path is first-class and there are two or three moving moments in the scenery. But overall it's too dull and too easy.

![](/images/2007/09/13/1443459297.jpg)

I hope Hungary will be better, and I'm toying with the idea of going home with Riki on Sunday.

Somehow I'd imagined this holiday would be more exciting.

From Linz I take the fast IC train to Vienna. My first impression of Vienna is loud and dusty – which turns out to be wrong: exploring with Riki, we discover a wonderful city with enchanting corners, streets and palaces that cast their spell on us.

We have a tasty Italian dinner (meat for the first time in ages!) and then move into our big apartment at the _Pension Kraml_.

Before that, we explore the city by night. A dream.
