## Tuesday, 18 September 2007

## Komárom – Esztergom 😃 ☀

My legs are tired and I'm wary of the hills. The weather looks good. The sun is shining and it's dry.

![](/images/2007/09/18/1443469527.jpg)

I made it to just past Vác: I rode on into the dark, and a Hungarian family (László Jambor) kindly let me stay the night.

What a blessing. I don't think I've ever been so scared and homesick.

Thank you, László Jambor!
