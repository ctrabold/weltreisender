## Monday, 19 April 2010

Durian Durian!

​19 April

Kota Bharu is brilliant. The weather is pleasantly mild and I can explore the town at leisure on my bike.

At the fruit market I load up my panniers: for next to nothing I get a kilo of super-juicy mangoes, a small watermelon and two fresh durians.

It's the first time I've bought durian on my own and as a whole fruit (always pre-packed before).

Buying durian is a science in itself and takes some practice. As I learnt in Bangkok, I check the smell, the firmness and the area around the stalk: all looks good. The stallholder nods approvingly too - if a little bewildered. I don't think she's ever had the chance to watch a Westerner buy a durian.

Durian is actually rather unpopular with Westerners, but that's very likely because their first durian experience was off-putting, or downright repellent, owing to a lack of quality awareness. It's best to buy (and enjoy!) durian with a local, far from the tourist strips!

My debut is a complete success: thanks to my Opinel knife, the fruit is open in a few moves and reveals gorgeous flesh.

The stones are only small to medium-sized, and the taste, thanks to the generous flesh, is once again indescribable. The fruit itself smells fantastically fresh too.

Proud as Punch, I dream my way, full, into sleep. Durian, you're amazing and I'm going to miss you terribly!
