## Thursday, 29 April 2010

Perhentian Islands Dance!

My days on the [Perhentian Islands](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perhentian_Islands "it really does look like that") are short and sweet.

I like island life there a bit more than on Ko Tao. It's (even) less hectic, the music more subdued and - apart from the Chinese tour groups at the dinner table - quieter and a bit more "organised". Sadly, the dive centre is only so-so and visibility underwater is very poor.

After a week of island life I go back to Kota Bharu, where I catch the night train to Singapore.

As I'm spicing up my meal in the station restaurant, I hear myself humming:

_Dance [sambal](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sambal) with me, sambal all night long_... (a pun on the German pop song "Tanze Samba mit mir")

Er, yes... It's high time I got out of the sun! The heat of the last few days has clearly left its mark.

A few hours later I'm rolling south through the night in an air-conditioned (deep-freeze) carriage.

The compartment is full, someone is apparently smoking right in front of the air-conditioning intake, and on the huge LCD screen some very hard men are butchering each other. The film "[The Tournament](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0471041/)" is a big hit above all with the toddlers, who follow every gush of blood on the TV with rapt attention. The adults doze, exchange tender looks or tend the nest (laying a blanket over their partner, plumping a pillow. I never once saw "skin on skin" in Malaysia).

The film is grating. A lousy copy of "[The Running Man](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093894/)". Really cheap action. The only good thing: the [lead](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000609/) is also in the [Dawn of the Dead remake](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0363547/), which reminds me that I absolutely must watch that film when I'm back home.

Anyone fancy joining me?
