## Wednesday, 6 January 2010

Night Bus Trauma

![Inside a Sleeper Bus // Im Sleeper-Bus](/images/2010/01/05/4258486535.jpg)The engine is already running when a mother squeezes onto the bus with her baby. Of course she takes the bed right next to me... Scream!

The bus engine is very loud, everything vibrates, the beds are optimised for Chinese people and sleep is pretty much out of the question. It's more that at some point my eyes close from exhaustion, and a few minutes later I'm rudely dragged back into the here and now by cramp in my legs, the next bus stop (all lights on!) or the baby screaming next to me.

Shortly after five the engine stops and I jolt awake: lots of people get off, a few stay lying down. Are we there? The drivers don't understand a word of English, stare silently at the map and just nod at each other. I decide simply to go back to my bed and stay there until someone throws me out. Maybe I can get a bit more sleep that way.

As I later find out, the bus really has arrived in Jinghong. Anyone who wants to can sleep on the bus until it gets light.

Shortly after eight it's light enough and I venture outside. It's already noticeably warm and much more humid than in Kunming.

I roll calmly through the town to the bridge, where my first view of the Mekong takes my breath away. The morning sun and the clouds make a fantastic picture! Despite the gruelling journey here, I'm glad I chose the bus.

After a quick breakfast and some shopping, I set off in high spirits towards Mengla. The road rolls nicely and the scenery makes me forget all the hardships.
