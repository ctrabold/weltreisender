## Friday, 22 January 2010

Canoeing!

Oh no: the weather's turning! Despite the dry season, heavy rain sweeps across the country. The roads become as slippery as glass, and several times Laotian scooters fly off the road on the bends. It makes cycling harder too.

Compared with Luang Prabang, Pakse is even more humid, and louder and busier too.

After an unusually long search, I finally find a room at the Hotel Paksé. It's affordable and nicely furnished. The view of the Mekong and the mountains is rather lovely as well.

The next morning I book a kayak trip on the Mekong. For three days we'll head south along the mighty river. On day three Rondolf will be sent on after me, luggage and all, and I'll carry on by bike to Cambodia - final destination Phnom Penh.

That's the plan, anyway. For now I'm very curious to see how I like my paddling debut.
