## Sunday, 17 January 2010

Houei Xai -> Luang Prabang

In the VIP Jeep

Feeling rotten. The trip here cost over 200 EUR and, all things considered, was a right pain. Sure, it had its upside too, being driven around the countryside in a jeep.

But right now I feel well and truly ripped off, because it was by no means much cheaper (!) or safer than the speedboats!

Blah blah blah: I've fallen for it again - someone giving me "something good, just like that" because I'm so wonderful.

Oh, why am I even worrying about this? Why is my mind so clogged up with silly thoughts? I have a plan, and I'm sticking to it brilliantly right now. Money is more of a side issue. In return I'm seeing amazing things and getting fascinating insights into the country that 99.9% of visitors never get.

That's the way to look at it.

On Thursday I could take a flight to Savannakhet - or Pakxe? I'll sort that out tomorrow and have a look round the town while I'm at it.

I'll change guesthouse too.
