## Saturday, 9 January 2010

Bye bye China!

{{< gallery from="2010-01-09" >}}

It's only a few more kilometres to the border. Tomorrow I leave China.

I'm doing well, and the ride through the new landscape is incredible. Very green, and impressively varied - especially compared with the endless concrete grey of the last few weeks.

The roads are noticeably quieter, and cycling in these new surroundings is great fun! The people are brilliant too, cheering me on from their mopeds when I overtake them or they whizz past me. Keyword: infectious laughter - how I've missed that!

Right now it's a mild 20 degrees, around 30 during the day, and I've got a bit of colour in my face again.

The last few weeks have been hard to beat for new impressions - China really is incredibly varied, not to mention the change of landscape and climate between Kunming, Guilin, Yangshuo and Xishuangbanna!

The train journey to Guilin alone would be worth a report of its own. As soon as I find an internet café in Laos, I'll write in more detail.
