## Monday, 25 January 2010

Arrival in Phnom Penh

It's just after 11 pm - we're finally here!

The city greets me with oppressive humidity and a crowd of tuk-tuk drivers wanting to take me to the best hotel in town.

As calmly as possible, I try to put the bike back together in the pale lamplight (I had to take off the front wheel) and straighten the mudguards. Something seems to have got bent on the journey.

I'll look for the cause tomorrow in daylight. Sweat is dripping from my forehead, and meanwhile Stephan has "found" a moped rider who'll take us for free to a guesthouse with a lake view. The prices sound fair, and we can look at the rooms with no obligation.

I follow the moped as inconspicuously as possible. The alleys are narrow and winding. Every few metres, shady characters peer out of the darkness and make even shadier offers.

The room really does have a lake view and is actually fairly quiet, even though the guesthouse is right in the heart of the falang (= foreigner) district.

I don't have the energy to complain about anything anyway. I'm glad to finally stretch my legs, and I enjoy the fan blowing cool air over my sweat-soaked trousers while my shirt slowly dries. Pleasantly cooled, I drift off to dreamland seconds later.
