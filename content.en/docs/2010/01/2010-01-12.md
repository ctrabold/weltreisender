## Tuesday, 12 January 2010

Major Dataquake in Luang Namtha, Laos

At 12:48 South-East Asia time, Luang Namtha in northern Laos was hit by one of the most severe dataquakes in the history of the Laotian internet. Major internet services such as Facebook and Flickr were unreachable for several minutes.

The latest geo-measurements place the epicentre at _18a Muang Sing Road_. Eyewitnesses report a white, bearded man with a typically Western mop of hair who disappeared into an internet café and uploaded pictures to a so-called photo-sharing site. Seconds later the network collapsed and the region's digital life ground to a halt.

At the press conference called afterwards, the man confessed: "I only wanted to upload a few pictures, and suddenly nothing worked any more!"

Rescue teams are currently working feverishly to dig the lines free of the accumulated data debris. The shimmering midday heat is making the rescue work even harder.

It remains unclear when normal service will be restored.

The alleged culprit is currently under observation at the Manychan Restaurant, where he is being sedated with Beerlao.
