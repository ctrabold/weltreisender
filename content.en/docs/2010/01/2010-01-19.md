## Tuesday, 19 January 2010

Just a Quick One - Nail Care and More in Luang Prabang

I like Luang Prabang so much that I spend four days there. A pleasant mix of liveliness and "easy-going" floats through the alleys, and the time flies by.

On the second day I also meet up with Oliver again. We first met on my first day in Laos at the first crossroads before Luang Namtha, where we promptly turned the little Laotian inn into a proper drinking session.

Together we explore Luang Prabang, the lively market and the fascinating silk weaving in the surrounding area. On the 19th we celebrate my eighth month "on tour" with Jimmy, the funny Taiwanese guy. Lovely!

The town's varied restaurants invite you to linger in the sun for hours. We give in to this temptation passionately, enjoying the good Laotian food on the side.

Towards evening I get my first taste of Laotian foot massage and nail care. The massage is good, but the nail care afterwards turns out to be far more radical than I'd wanted.

Snip snip, all my fingernails are cut right down to the skin, so in the evening I struggle to change my tyres and pack my panniers.

Tomorrow I'm heading towards Pakse in southern Laos.
