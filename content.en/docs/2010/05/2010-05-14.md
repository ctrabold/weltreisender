## Friday, 14 May 2010

Colourful Singapore

My days in Singapore are flying by.

Maybe because my departure date is inevitably getting closer and the tension rises every day. This mixture of anticipation and the pain of leaving is incredibly hard to describe, and often really tests my patience.

Good thing there are plenty of distractions in Singapore:

It's always pleasantly warm, and everywhere smells of jasmine and patchouli trees. The nightlife is pleasant and I have some intense nights in the city's clubs. At Ralph's apartment complex the dolphin fountains splash in the pool. I really let off steam in the gym and the sauna.

Day by day I wind down my "travel mode" a little more and prepare for my return by flat-hunting and doing other admin.

On the day of departure I'm surprisingly composed. After hours of trying to pack my bike into a bike box, I discover that the box is too small after all. With oil-smeared hands I screw it back together and order a MaxiTaxi.

Rondolf rides along in the boot, perfectly normally. As always! With the taxi driver I run through the highlights of my journey once more at top speed.

At the airport it's all no problem. A friendly security officer collects the bike and it travels free of charge (as it's under 15 kilos). My luggage, too, at 19 kilos, is just under the free allowance.

Smooth takes on a whole new meaning here.

What's more, Changi Airport is really very quiet and relaxed. Perfectly signposted, no queues, friendly security check only shortly before departure. And I've still got enough time for a super sushi snack.

When we take off I'm composed - far more so than on any other departure so far. A new stage of the journey is beginning. And I accept that.

I gave it my all once more over the last few days and regret nothing. Secretly I may even be looking forward to a bit more calm and composure in life.

I'm curious.
