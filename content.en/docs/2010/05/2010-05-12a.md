## Wednesday, 12 May 2010

Regular at the App Store

As I've mentioned a few times, the iPod Touch has turned into a really valuable travel companion on my journey.

Being able to load music and games independently of iTunes is especially great.

Here's the list of apps I used:

The top 5 pre-installed apps:

1. Mail - among other things, lots of blog posts were written in Mail
2. Safari
3. Clock (in world clock mode)
4. Weather
5. Contacts

The top 5 apps I installed:

1. eCurrency, for quick currency conversion
2. Pzizz Relax, for relaxing in between
3. Pzizz Sleep, for a good night's sleep in dorms
4. Skype, for the "good night!" chat with home.
5. WifiTrak, for finding WiFi networks
