## Sunday, 16 May 2010

Dreary Germany

Before landing we pass through three thick layers of cloud. Only then does German soil come into view. Fog everywhere, motorways, concrete buildings. The sun doesn't stand a chance.

When Frankfurt's skyline appears shortly before landing, I can't help laughing. Aw, how cute!

The captain's announcement confirms my suspicions about the temperatures on the ground: a difference of more than 20 degrees Celsius.

Calmly I put on my thick fleece jumper, which I haven't worn since the ice-cold slither through China's mountains (before Xichang), and prepare as best I can for the impending cold shock.

A calm flight comes to an end - faster than I expected. Only occasionally is there a little turbulence. Otherwise, through the dead of night, we fly over a small stretch of my journey once more (Black Sea, Ukraine, Slovakia, Poland).

While everyone sleeps, I try to get used to German time already and bravely keep my eyes open, listen to music and watch out of the window as huge towers of cloud flash not far away. When my eyes close, I sleep deeply and peacefully and wake up rested.

At the airport, the customs officer's curt "Moin" finally brings me back to Germany. After I've collected my bike, I get a warm welcome from my loved ones. The reunion is wonderful and suddenly I'm not cold at all any more. Thank you!

After a funny car journey ("They're all driving on the wrong side!") with an impromptu city tour ("It's all just the same as ever"), at last the hot shower at home: my shampoo, my shower gel. All back again! And at last, at long last – after 12 months – putting on my own fresh jeans and a normal T-shirt! A really special feeling!

We have breakfast until late in the evening. Good bread, cheese and cold cuts cushion the culinary changeover. The champagne gets the circulation going and I get through the highs and lows of jet lag well.

Still: a large part of my heart is trailing many hours and kilometres behind. The whole week is one big emotional rollercoaster and truly indescribable.

I'm looking forward to seeing you all again, looking forward to my new flat, exciting projects and so much more.
