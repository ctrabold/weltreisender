###### Things to Remember

- Always smile and enjoy
- _Always_ close panniers properly (against water, damp, ants etc.)
- Always _hang_ rubbish in a separate bag _outside_ the tent. Ants are always everywhere.
- Drink plenty (no alcohol)
- Always close the mosquito net as quickly as possible
- Top up water in the evening - not in the morning in crowded showers or washrooms
- Wash clothes every three days at most. The damp stays and is more unpleasant than the smell - especially when it's cooler outside.
- Go without as often as possible, and then enjoy to the full!
- There are no shortcuts and there are no detours
- Put on rain gear as early as possible, not when it's already spitting.
- Put on the full set of rain gear, and don't hope it'll pass over in a minute anyway. Damp clothes are the worst!
- How about you stop now and get started? (me to myself)
- The art is to be content with simple answers too (Solenice 04.06.09)
- Scan your signature so you can sign contracts such as crossed cheques "in your own hand" from afar
- In a hotel: always hang the red (do not disturb) sign on the door first (I've been rudely woken by the cleaners too often… not funny. Bye-bye, night's sleep)
- "On into the new day" - idea for a book title while pondering the time differences
- Christian SPAbold
- Sun is awesome
- Digital thermometer is better than mercury
- Effervescent tablets turn to powder and become unusable. Capsules (fish oil) go soft, ORS sachets clump. Packed wrongly?
- A solar charger can make sense. Better than (flat) batteries
- Drip scented oil onto your neckerchief for public toilets (collect herbs by the roadside?)
- A laser pointer avoids misunderstandings in shops, but also avoids funny situations. Pointing directly with a finger led to the wrong choice 99% of the time
- Business card with name, URL for blog and photos. QR code? Constantly writing it down gets a bit tedious
- Make sure zips are made in one piece. On 17.08.09 the plastic tab came off my Rotwild jersey, so the zip is now very hard to open and close
- A spork is no good for scraping out mugs
- Never pack food and clothes together
- The Ortlieb water bag closure is daft. The main cap should open _clockwise_, the small one _anticlockwise_. So often I opened the whole load of water with the small cap
- If honey, then set honey. Runny honey likes to leak
- If possible, get up-to-date maps locally. The Chinese maps from Globetrotter (a German outdoor shop) show completely different roads. Age difference: 1 year
- A powerful horn would make sense now and then. In many countries a bicycle bell doesn't register as a warning signal, it's more amusing
- Ringing is pointless. Braking is always better
- Cocoa powder would have been great in China. Chocolate there is disgusting. There's no real chocolate here.
- Pictures from home to show people
- China: the "happy spray-mobile" sprays the road wet to a jolly tune. Likewise the tootling bin lorry. Very different. I miss it.
- Book idea: look at the trip from both sides and document how those left at home deal with the traveller (= tips for both sides)
- Spare seal for earphones. If one is missing, the whole music fun is gone. Can't buy them individually.
- iPod headphones are utter rubbish
- NEVER rely on plastic. It always breaks.
- Riding in long sleeves with a Merino Icebreaker 150 is very comfortable and doubles as sun protection. Sadly the shirt wears out quickly. Holes have torn.
