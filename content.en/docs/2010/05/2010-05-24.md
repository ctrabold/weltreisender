## Monday, 24 May 2010

Pudding, Pudding, Hourglass

A week in Germany is coming to an end. Here's a quick summary of the most important things.

What's great:

1. I've got a new flat in Frankfurt
2. There were three extended (or rather, waist-extending) barbecues with my loved ones in glorious weather!
3. Cologne Cathedral is still in its rightful place. I checked personally.
   And the people of Cologne still know how to party properly. Very reassuring! Thanks for a fantastic weekend!
4. There are real, good books again! Books! Books! Thanks for the warm welcome @ [O'Reilly publishers](https://www.oreilly.de/)!
5. Pudding! ... yes, there are some things I have to taste again to realise how much I've missed them.
6. My old running route is just as great as it used to be! JP Morgan, here I come!

What I didn't miss at all over the last few months:

1. the silly smoking in clubs and restaurants, and above all the careless handling of cigarettes on the dance floor.
2. the hourglass on my computer. Isn't it pleased at all that I'm back? =:-O
3. cycle paths crowded with pedestrians. If you're going to do it, do it properly.
4. oh yes: and stag dos :)

I'm sleeping well again and feel at home in my old home town. All good!
