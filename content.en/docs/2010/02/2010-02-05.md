## Friday, 5 February 2010

You Make Your Bed

​4 to 5 February

At last I tear myself away from Phnom Penh!

The ride through the city is loud, hot and fast. As expected, once I'm past the city limits, wide fields line the roadside. Not a scrap of shade. The sun can beat down on my head at full strength.

Road Number 4 is narrow, busy and noisy, but in good condition. The first 70 kilometres roll by nicely - even without breakfast.

But when nothing edible appears on my table at the third attempt, I do start to get cross. Noodle soup or any other halfway substantial meal is out of the question, even after persistent asking (with hands and feet).

Instead, the choice is between various offal stews quietly festering in the heat. Given the temperatures, I skip any experiments and try to refill my energy reserves with lychee water and my tried-and-tested Sprite.

At around 2 pm I bed down under a tree for a nap. But only half an hour later I wake with a start. Someone is talking to me in a voice that's breaking. Damn!

It's the boy who's been "following" me on his moped for several kilometres and whom I disliked from the very first moment. Again and again he overtakes me on his clapped-out old rattletrap, gawping at me with a stupid grin. Then he waits in the shade of the next tree until I ride past. And the game starts all over again - at least six times. Sometimes I really don't know what goes on in some people's heads...

Now he's crept up on me and is trying to start a conversation. Anyone who disturbs my nap needs a good reason, and he hasn't got one. He babbles something (probably the same unintelligible rubbish as everyone else).

I ignore him and close my eyes again. Or hang on: what did he just say? "Do you want to ... fuck?" Did I hear that right?

A long silence. Then he has another go. Despite all the honking, my ears are evidently still pretty good, and my suspicion is confirmed. I sit up, glare at him and try to shoo him away. He's persistent and doesn't budge. Thoroughly fed up, I ride on. He stays put for a while, then grumbles his way back. What on earth was that about?

On every corner here there are signs pointing out that "sex with children" is - of course - illegal in Cambodia too. If you spot a "couple" like that, you're supposed to call a number. Civil courage is expected.

At some point I reach a town (there are no town signs) and turn off onto the side road, hoping I've found Road 48 to the hotel. Sure enough, the hotel is signposted a few metres further on: _Turn right at the second bridge_.

I do as I'm told and follow the quiet road. Things get much more relaxed. The evening sun lays its soft light over the green fields and it gets hillier. When I reach the second bridge, there really are smart houses by the roadside. At a checkpoint I ask about the price: 50 dollars a night. The official explains that there's a cheaper hotel further up, so I ride on.

Now it's a steep climb. My jersey and gloves are as wet as if I'd been swimming in them. All around me things are buzzing and whirring again. Great! Eventually I can't go on and hang my mosquito net from a little wooden hut by the side of the road.

The night is very noisy and restless. My things stay wet and the net really ought to hang much higher... so I keep getting tangled up in it and pulling it off.

At some point I do fall asleep - with practically no protection from mosquitoes - and wake up again shortly after 6. I'm thoroughly crumpled, but glad the night is over. Not a single mosquito bite. But plenty of ant bites - the ants evidently couldn't care less about the Autan (insect repellent).

Memories of the last big ride through the Chinese mountains come flooding back. Only this time it's toasty warm. Even at night it barely cools down and is at least as muggy as during the day.

After more steep kilometres I reach the hotel in question, and ride past it all the way to the summit. At the top the road ends and I enjoy the view down into the valley. The nature reserve is really wonderful!

As there's no road onwards, I turn round and carry on along the 4 to Sre Ambel. According to the map, there should be a hotel there too.
