## Monday, 8 February 2010

Switching Off in Koh Kong

​6 to 8 February

My days in Koh Kong are mainly about recovery. Despite drinking regularly, I'm pretty dehydrated and feel a bit rough.

So much for switching off! The undisputed highlight of these days is (surprisingly) the local TV channel RTV-Germany, put together by Germans. On Sunday evening there's actually _Tatort_ (Germany's cult Sunday-night crime series) - three episodes back to back!

After that, "Back to the Future" and "12 Monkeys" keep the mood in my room going until the small hours.

The ad breaks between the films are very odd. They "advertise" beach bars and discos in typical cinema-ad style. Anyone who knows the cinema ads for the Theke bar in Darmstadt, Germany, will have a rough idea of the scale.
