## Thursday, 4 February 2010

From Phnom Penh to Koh Kong in three days

​2 to 4 February

Leaving Phnom Penh is hard.

When I get back to Phnom Penh from Siem Reap, I happen to run into Christian.

We spend a convivial few hours together in a pub not far from the Mekong, and afterwards sit on the balcony of our hotel until sunrise. Time flies. Unbelievable.

Still, all this lazing about is wearing me down, and on 4 February - a day later than planned - I set off towards the Thai border.

I'm allowing three days for the "Phnom Penh - Kirirom - Sre Ambel - Koh Kong" stretch.

My first target is a hotel near Kirirom, just under 100 kilometres away. Let's see if I make it, and how cycling feels after such a long break.

Raring to go, I set off at around 11 o'clock. Back in the saddle at last!
