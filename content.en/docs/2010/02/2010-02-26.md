## Friday, 26 February 2010

## Couchsurfing in Bangkok or Southern Thailand?

24 to 26 February

Shortly after I've got my ticket to Surat Thani in my pocket, an email invitation flutters in for the "CouchSurfing get-together Bangkok".

The train leaves tomorrow evening, so I decide to check out the meet-up and the couchsurfers (CS).

The meeting point is only a few metres from the hotel, and I set off, full of anticipation, to my first CS event.

At the get-together I meet - as expected - lots of travellers and locals. There's - as expected - the usual small talk.

Far more surprising is that, at some point, somehow, I "arrive" back at the hotel in the morning after a very funny night. Lovely!

The next day we gather in front of the Bangkok International Museum to look at some Japanese artists.

There I happen to meet Toom, who then invites me along to a group meal at a restaurant.

My first experience of CouchSurfing couldn't be better. The food is superb and the conversations in the smaller group deep and good. At first I'm a bit unsure, but it just keeps getting better.

Toom invites me to stay on his couch, and I follow him somewhere in the dead of night. As I discover the next morning, he's hosting nine couchsurfers in his house.

Breakfast together with all these travellers from around the world, in a little Thai restaurant on the street, turns into a real feast - especially compared with the lonely, rather forced breakfasts at the hotel.

Shortly after breakfast the train ticket lies torn up in the bin, and in the evening Toom, Matthew, Ward, Eny and I check out the BBQ at the shopping centre.

My mind is made up: I'm staying a bit longer.
