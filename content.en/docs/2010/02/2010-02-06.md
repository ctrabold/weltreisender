## Saturday, 6 February 2010

Mountain Stage Through the Fragrant Cardamom Mountains

​6 February

The ride to Sre Ambel was long and hot. But I find a hotel with a shower and a clean bed.

Sadly, the biggest problem is still getting hold of food.

In one restaurant I have to hand one of my packet soups over to the kitchen so they can make it up with hot water. Otherwise I'd have had to make do with raw Khmer veg.

In the evening, in a little restaurant not far from the hotel, I finally find noodle soup without touching my emergency rations - or so it seems. What lands on my plate is three packets of instant noodles with a bit of greenery. Pepped up with plenty of chilli sauce, I can just about eat it. A culinary wasteland.

And I need every carbohydrate going for tomorrow, because 140 kilometres are on the cards! The final stage to Koh Kong, through the Cardamom Mountains.

Getting away from Sre Ambel the next morning is slow going. I miss the turning for the main road and end up riding cross-country through Cambodian villages instead. Nice for a change!

After a few friendly chats with the villagers, I roll leisurely onto Road Number 48. It's superbly surfaced and has very little traffic. A few clouds cover the sun, so it stays fairly cool until 11 o'clock. What's more, every town has little shops and even proper restaurants, where I finally get my well-earned noodle soup!

I stop at every refuelling point, because the route profile is quite something. It's constantly uphill, then a bit downhill, which is utterly exhausting. I drink almost 8 litres of water that day.

Even so, the beauty of nature and the peace win out. In pitch darkness I battle my way up the last hills and then roll into Koh Kong, relieved and overjoyed.
