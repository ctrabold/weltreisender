## What happens next?

Every now and then I look for traces of the Christian from before the trip. Some of my friends say I've changed a lot since the trip, or because of it. I can't tell, and it doesn't really matter to me. I feel good.

Whenever I think about the trip, pictures flash through my head straight away.

Right after I got back, that happened without my even thinking about it. Later only just before falling asleep, often with fits of crying.

The hot tears of longing make the pictures even sharper. Pictures turn into films, and films into experiences that I miss.

The pride in what I've achieved feels more like a hole. Thoughts of loss instead of pride and good feelings.

After the journey, the real journey begins. It's true. And all happiness lies in the now. Enjoy it. Now.

Happy travels!

Yours, Christian
