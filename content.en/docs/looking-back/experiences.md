## Managing photos

### Some thoughts first

A question that was on my mind long before the trip: how do I keep on top of all those travel photos?

My approach

1. First of all I signed up for a Flickr [Pro account](http://www.flickr.com/help/limits/#28). That gives me all the features plus unlimited storage. (Note: no longer necessary)

2. Every evening before bed I delete the blurry, unflattering and shaky shots from my camera's memory card.

   A nice side effect: it makes me automatically go over the day again, and it saves time when uploading, too.

3. For each country I create a [collection](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/collections/) (Pro accounts only). Into it go the [albums](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/sets/) I create for each stage and/or event.

   Albums with photos of people I've met on my travels have worked really well. I can share those individually via a [guest pass link](http://www.flickr.com/help/guestpass/#1351610).

4. Put the pictures on the world map as soon as possible! Later on it's hard to work out where they were taken. A rough position is better than none at all!

5. Add keywords (="[tagging](http://www.flickr.com/help/tags/#37)")

   I tag the pictures in several steps:

   1. After uploading, I add general standard tags (e.g. [travel](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/tags/travel), [Asia](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/tags/asia)).

   1. Then, in the Organizr, I add tags that fit the theme ([name of the city](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/tags/hongkong), [group](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/tags/group), [traffic](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/tags/traffic)). This is also when I add people to the pictures. Pictures I'm in get the keyword "[me](http://www.flickr.com/photos/christian-trabold/tags/me)".

   1. As a basis for tagging I use these ideas from the Flickr forum:
      [http://www.flickr.com/groups/central/discuss/2026/](http://www.flickr.com/groups/central/discuss/2026/)
      [http://www.flickr.com/groups/central/discuss/2730/](http://www.flickr.com/groups/central/discuss/2730/)

6. Add a description and a title!

   Thanks to the albums and the positions on the map, I can recall the situation much better - even much later.

   A title and description can then be added fairly easily, independently of the earlier steps. Diary notes help jog the memory as well. Even so, giving each picture a title and description is by far the most time-consuming part.

7. Publish the pictures using Flickr's privacy settings. Family and friends (sometimes) get to see more pictures than the rest of the "world".

8. Optional: if I think a picture might appeal to other Flickr members too, I submit it to a suitable [photo group](http://www.flickr.com/groups/).

### Verdict

So far this workflow has worked really well. Managing the pictures takes a huge amount of time (I've spent whole days in internet cafés doing it - not to mention uploading them), BUT: even though I'm already managing over 6,000 pictures on Flickr, I still have everything under control. The tags in particular give me amazingly fast and intuitive access to moments gone by.

And I can share those moments with you... and isn't that what photography is all about?
