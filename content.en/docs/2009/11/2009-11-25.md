## Wednesday, 25 November 2009

Hong Kong in a Nutshell

Wow! I've been here a week already - unbelievable!

Pictures: [Hong Kong]({{< relref "/tags/hong-kong" >}})

The days are flying by faster than they have in a long time. Life here is exciting and fast. There's an incredible amount to see - even if you're "just" strolling through the streets. I also meet up with Daniel and we paint the town red together! I've still got it!! :)

For me, Hong Kong is like London, only "taller". The skyscrapers are everywhere. So is the British touch. It's "very British" with a generous pinch of "Asia" - a mixture I find very pleasant and refreshing.

The nights are short, partly thanks to the fast (!) internet. At last I can manage my pictures on Flickr properly and upload the videos that almost drove me mad over the slow connection in China. I've also set up a Facebook account and added my travel contacts, among other things. Your tweets make for great reading too! Nice to see that things are running smoothly at home as well!

All in all, my trip to Hong Kong is like a little holiday from the holiday. The never-ending thoughts about visa extensions, route planning and finding somewhere to sleep have vanished for a while, and - smog or no smog - I can finally take a deep, Western breath again.

I'm in a place where everything just works beautifully and where there's so much to see that I could easily use up my 90-day visa. The public transport system is a dream, and so are the food and the weather. Despite the language barrier, I find my way around straight away, and there's cheap, very tasty food everywhere. Usually there's room for a little ice cream for dessert, too. A city to grow round in!

The bookshops are excellently stocked as well, and I get all the important maps for the next leg of my journey.

This visit has completely changed the way I see Hong Kong. It's far more than just a big city with tall buildings! Instead, it presents an incredibly varied landscape with a surprising amount of green, mixed with the deep blue of the South China Sea.

I particularly like Lamma Island. There I watch a fantastic sunset over the South China Sea. A night-time boat trip takes us back to Hong Kong Central, past the imposing skyline.
