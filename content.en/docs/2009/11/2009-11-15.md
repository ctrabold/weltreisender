## Sunday, 15 November 2009

Gamers of the World - Unite!

Chengdu, 15 November

{{< gallery from="2009-11-14" to="2009-11-15" >}}

Today we're off to the _World Cyber Games_ in Chengdu. THE world championship of computer gaming! I'm curious. After such a short night, the spectacle feels even bigger. %-)

We get past the long queue, and a few minutes later we're squeezed in with thousands of other Asians in front of the giant screen in the packed hall, waiting for the first match.

Months earlier the qualifiers were held in each country, this week the qualifying rounds were fought out in Chengdu, and today is "finals" day! Today the real gaming heroes go head to head. The air is thick with tension right up to the ceiling.

On the programme: FIFA 2009 (= football simulation, one on one), CounterStrike (= shooter, team of 5 against team of 5), StarCraft (= strategy, one on one) and WarCraft (= strategy, one on one).

The very first match brings a surprise: the football final is contested by two Germans. Germany versus Germany! How lovely!

The match is incredibly exciting - almost like a real game - and the hall goes wild like a football stadium. Brilliant!

After that, the stars from Korea battle it out among themselves for the StarCraft world title.

To round things off there's an all-Chinese final in WarCraft. The hall keeps filling up and erupts at every attack or counter-attack.

The players are celebrated like stars here. There are real fans, posters held up in the air, reporters commentating on the moves! A real media spectacle.

Hugely impressed and thoroughly puzzled, we leave the halls in the evening and get back to the hostel a few hours later.

Tomorrow Ethan and his brother fly back to Shanghai. Over a beer in the hostel lounge we wind down after some lovely days together.
