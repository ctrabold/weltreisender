## Monday, 2 November 2009

Full Steam to Chengdu

Hooray!

Over the last few stages I've racked up so many bonus miles that I'm now allowed to take the train for the stretch to Chengdu. ;-p

From Chengdu I'll carry on by bike to Kunming.

We leave tomorrow morning at 9:24 local time (while you're hopefully still fast asleep). I checked my bike in at the freight counter today. Quite an adventure, but it all went without a hitch.

Why the train? I'm hoping to get to the southern part of China a bit faster. Other travellers' photos and stories promise great things. Doing it by bike is simply too risky for me (time, cold). I'd like to arrive in Kunming on time, so I can extend my visa again there (twice should be possible), apply for visas for the next countries, and so on and so on.

The journey lasts until 10-ish the next day... so a good 25 hours. I'm curious to see how the Chinese build their trains, and I'll be in touch again from Chengdu.

Best wishes and speak soon! Yours, Chistrain
