## Saturday, 14 November 2009

## Flotte Beats and Disco Westerners

Chengdu, 14 November

Tonight Ethan, his brother and I set out to plunge into Chinese nightlife. Our options: the "bar" district, or Bar 88 a bit further out.

We try every club anyone has recommended, and they're all awful.

Maybe I'm (getting) too ol... no, too nervous about Monday's visa business. I keep drawing up plans B, C and D and just can't seem to relax.

Verdict: the nightlife leaves me cold. It's a cross between carnival, fairground and far too much booze, with strange rituals and dance-and-drink games. Chatting with the curious (and mostly rather tipsy) locals is impossible over the blaring sound system - and pretty tiresome anyway.

When a tiny, blind-drunk Chinese woman with garish fake hair tries to party with us, my mood sinks even lower. Flailing about, she knocks over our glasses and spills half of hers. And whoosh - she's gone, swallowed up by the heaving crowd. Cheers!

Luckily we find a little street restaurant, where we treat ourselves to a midnight snack and a good, cheap beer while our clothes dry off.

Readers from Cologne, Germany, will picture the scene the moment I say "Flotte". For everyone else: the "Flotte" is a carnival pub in Cologne's student quarter. Think dancing on the tables, far too many far too drunk young people, and so on. I watch plenty of Westerners letting rip. Aliens, indeed.

The music is out of tune, deafening and - oh - just awful, awful. On top of that, at some point I lose Ethan and end up on my own.

At four I grab a taxi back to the hostel and get an earful from the night porter for dragging him out of bed at that hour.

I'm hugely relieved when I'm finally in bed and can savour the ringing in my ears in peace.

A few hours later we're in a taxi heading south - a bit worse for wear, but all present and correct - for the finals of the World Cyber Games!
