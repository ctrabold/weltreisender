## Monday, 9 November 2009

Emei for You!

Today I set off from Heilongtan towards Leshan. The Giant Buddha is calling!

As I step out of the hotel, I can hardly believe my eyes: fog!

At times visibility is well under 25 metres. Bends, mopeds and pedestrians appear out of nowhere.

In the mountains all around me, horns are honking in the white fog. Kind of funny, but also very tiring.

Most cars drive without lights - but with their hazard lights on and their HORNS blaring.

After more than 100 kilometres I reach Emei - just as darkness falls.

One thing reassures me: even in daylight I wouldn't have seen any of the surrounding mountains. It's very hazy all day.
