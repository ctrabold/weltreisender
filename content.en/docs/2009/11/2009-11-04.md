## Wednesday, 4 November 2009

A Chris Called Panda

Arrived safely in Chengdu. The journey was fun and flew by.

After almost two hours of waiting I can finally collect my bike at the "China Railway Express (!) Co., Ltd" counter. My beard is THE main attraction, which makes the wait a bit more bearable. ;)

Even more than the thousand questions from curious Chinese (in Chinese, Tibetan or Chinglish, take your pick), it's the new hot, muggy climate that's making me sweat.

It's a good 10 degrees warmer than in Xining, and the streets are even wider, busier and louder. Yippee!

I'm staying at a youth hostel (gogosg.com). The place is teeming with backpackers and other tourists.

I'm off for breakfast now (panda steak?) and to explore the neighbourhood. Speak soon!

Yours, Christian from Panda City
