## Monday, 27 April 2009

## Face to face with visa rules, viruses and travel health insurance

Things are slowly getting exciting:

The last jab is done, my kit is complete, and I've read the visa rules for Ukraine, Russia, India etc. _and_ understood them (honestly). The confirmation from my travel health insurance has arrived too.

With visa applications you have to be really careful about what you write where and how. You also need at least one passport photo per application. But I grind to a halt at the two fields "date of entry" and "date of exit", because:

_How on earth am I supposed to know my exact entry date already?_

According to a visa agency, the details really **do** have to be very precise. Rough dates aren't an option...

I'll sort out exactly how to handle this with the visa agency (and report back here).
