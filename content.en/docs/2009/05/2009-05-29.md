## Friday, 29 May 2009

Hotel-hopping in the Egerland

What a night: shortly after I've fallen asleep in the "Prima" hotel, a horde of Czech choirboys and their entourage descends on the hotel. Thoroughly drunk, they perform one or two courtship serenades in the corridor and the room next door before finally passing out (luckily no further courtship activities follow - the thin walls would have let every detail through).

Even so, I lie awake until half past four listening to music, and at around half past nine I stroll down to the breakfast "buffet".

Breakfast wasn't so prima and sits in my stomach like a stone. On top of that, the rain clouds have pushed in front of the sun again. The route is very confusingly signposted and I very nearly ride straight past Karlsbad. How's that possible? You stubbornly keep your eyes on cycle route 372, try at the same time to work out where you are on the map (so you don't miss the town, of all things) and, all the while, smilingly dodge dogs, oncoming cyclists, skaters etc. (who aren't exactly accommodating...).

Add to that the queasy feeling in my stomach, the sh**ty weather and plain frustration that Karlsbad isn't all that great.

As it turns out later, I'd been weaving my way round the outskirts of Karlsbad…

I pluck up my courage and leave the cycle path, heading for the centre of KV. Sadly, today neither the fine Karlsbad pralines nor the Karlsbad wafers can lift my mood, and after a measly 15 kilometres I collapse into the Egerländer guesthouse.

I book the room for two nights straight away and hope I'll finally be able to recover properly. Maybe the rain will pass, too.
