## Thursday, 28 May 2009

## From BT to CZ

![](/images/2009/05/28/3610620660.jpg)

Ahoj!

The ride to the Czech Republic dragged on like chewing gum. The mountains took a lot out of me. Then there was the storm with strong winds... but the Fichtelgebirge mountains are really, really worth seeing! The stretch through the Fichtel valley is by far one of the most beautiful I've ever ridden. Alongside a rushing stream, through wonderfully fragrant mixed woodland, always gently uphill.

My legs are fit, so yesterday I flew across the open "green border" into the Czech Republic. I only noticed because the cycle signs looked different.

In Cheb I found a very nice, cheap guesthouse. Today I'm heading for Karlovy Vary (Karlsbad).

The first pictures are up on Flickr!
