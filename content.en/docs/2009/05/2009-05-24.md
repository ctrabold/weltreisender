## Sunday, 24 May 2009

## Smoked beer and 105 degrees Celsius

![](/images/2009/05/23/3572237151.jpg)

The path winds through lovely valleys - sometimes close to the Main, sometimes further away. The weather is glorious again and the sun is relentless.

The day starts at a gentle pace: for the first time in ages I've slept well, and at 8 o'clock I calmly pack my bags. Unlike usual, today I fill up all my water bottles, because the sweat is already trickling down under my jersey.

Then I put my foot down on the pedals, only to be rewarded with the first climb right after the first bend. The route is getting noticeably hillier. The landscape is opening up too.
I reach Bamberg around midday, which gives me plenty of time to run a few errands, drink some smoked beer (_Rauchbier_, a Bamberg speciality) and explore the town.

I pick up fresh batteries for my head torch, isotonic powder for the water bottles, a charger for the iPod and "fruit salad" gummy bears from the sweet shop.

The smoked beer goes down rather well, and the rest of the ride through the Coburg countryside is light work.

![](/images/2009/05/23/3572236915.jpg)

Shortly afterwards I reach Bad Staffelstein, Germany, and I soon know: I'm staying here longer. You see, there's a wonderful brine spa with a whole sauna complex where I can whip my battered muscles back into shape.

And so I spend Saturday evening and Sunday in this glorious place. Among other things, I get to know the Maa sauna, which is heated by a log fire and gets up to 105 degrees.

In case you're wondering whether it's wise to expose yourself to those temperatures with a sun-shrivelled head: I found it very pleasant. Only the after-sweat went on all night. I still slept very well.

The Main is now just a little stream, meandering through the wide open Coburg foothills.

Thanks to the rest day and the sauna I feel fresh and I'm looking forward to tomorrow.
