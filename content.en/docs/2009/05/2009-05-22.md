## Friday, 22 May 2009

## Good grief! All these hills?

Just after 4 in the morning I wake with a start and - in what's already a familiar routine - begin packing my bags and climbing out of the tent, when I realise that the dawn glow is actually a street lamp playing tricks on me.

Exhausted, I fall back into a restless sleep, which is abruptly interrupted by a deep rumble of thunder. And with it comes my tent's first baptism of rain. I have to postpone my planned departure.

At just after 10:30 I'm finally on my way towards Bamberg.

The road drags on. The rain seems to be rushing on ahead of me. The result: ankle-deep puddles and mud obstacle courses metres long. It's incredibly muggy - the road is steaming. On top of that, the route is getting hillier and hillier.

In Schweinfurt I lunch on half a plaited sweet loaf and a carrot. On the way to Haßfurt I notice I haven't got enough water with me. Running short of water really gets me down. Added to that, the next town is home to the people of Haßfurt (_Hass_ means "hate" in German). How might they react to strangers? A slight sense of unease creeps in.

The new HAS number plates are one of the first things I notice in Haßfurt.

I wonder which joker drives around with HASS or HASI ("bunny")? A few metres later the first HASI cuts me up in a silver Mercedes SLK convertible, pulls over and totters on high heels into some cheap little fashion boutique. Shortly afterwards I leave HAS behind.

Despite this amusing episode, the day stays unusually joyless for a Friday, so after just under 73 kilometres I head for the next campsite and put my tent out to dry in the warm evening sun.

With a cool shandy in hand, I send greetings and a hearty cheers back home and hope your Friday was more fun ;)
