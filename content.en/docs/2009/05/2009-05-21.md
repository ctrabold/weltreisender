## Thursday, 21 May 2009

## Rolling

![](/images/2009/05/21/3572235717.jpg)

Dear all,

the first few days have been brilliant. The bike is purring along, my legs are fit and the weather is glorious (and my backside only hurts a little). Every climb gets conquered at 6 km/h. I wonder what the hills will feel like once there's a 1 or a 2 in front of that ;)

The cycle path is a dream. Well surfaced, flat and hardly any traffic. Butterflies keep me company.

I'm still on the Main cycle path in Würzburg. The morning thunderstorm is moving off and the sky is clearing. Well rested, it's back on the bike now.

Speak soon!
