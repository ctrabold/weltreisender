## Thursday, 28 May 2009

## Ride from hell to Karlsbad

![](/images/2009/05/28/3609813227.jpg)

Even in the Czech Republic, you shouldn't count your chickens before they're hatched.

After the day started off extremely relaxed and amusing, with a long breakfast with Milan, my Czech host, the heavens opened at 4 pm: it poured down with every bucket St Peter could carry. The wind picked up considerably, too. No fun on the rather narrow roads here. Especially as there are often deep potholes lurking at the edge of the road.

Yet the first few metres on unfamiliar ground were pretty uneventful: thanks to my cycling map I quickly find my way around. The cycle route signs are easy to spot thanks to their bright yellow. They're also often put up well before the turn-off, so you can start your manoeuvre in good time. There's hardly anything more frustrating for a cyclist than whizzing past at full speed a sign telling you to turn off (which happened to me several times in Germany).

Unlike in Germany, every cycle route here has a number.

The first part of the ride takes me along the hilly roads before Karlovy Vary (Karlsbad). The second part runs through deep enchanted forest along the Ohře (Eger) river. I cross adventurous little bridges (barely wider than my handlebars) and tackle forest tracks that would easily pass for single trails back home.

At just before 7 pm - five kilometres short of Karlsbad - I check into the "Prima" hotel, dripping wet and pleasantly exhausted, move into my room and enjoy a 300g pepper steak with potato dumplings, assorted cold hop soups (beers) and the free Wi-Fi.

The sky is clearing - the view outside makes me hope for a decent day in Karlsbad. _Lookin' forward to it!_
