## Friday, 1 May 2009

## First test ride successfully completed

It's done. Today I finally got to do my first test ride on the fully loaded bike.

In glorious May weather I did a loop around Dieburg, Germany, with friends and (alongside a few cold beers) enjoyed the new feel of the bike.

The panniers are evenly packed and, at just under 20 kilos, I'm well within my weight limit (tools and spare parts still to come).

Even so, riding feels unfamiliar and a bit _spongy_ at first. The low-riders (front panniers) make the steering sluggish, and the rear panniers soak up even the most powerful push on the pedals.

As any proper test ride should, it also turned up a few things here and there that could be improved.

So I'd recommend every touring cyclist does a test ride as early as possible, to get "warmed up" with the loaded bike. There's a big difference between packing the bike _only_ in your head or "on checklists" and seeing the whole thing _for real_. Only then does the true scale of it really sink in. Only then does the packing really begin.
