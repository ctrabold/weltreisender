## Sunday, 31 May 2009

## A sunny day?

![](/images/2009/05/31/3609833509.jpg)

The view out of the window shows blue sky! I quickly pack my things and at just after 9 I say "adieu" to the Karlsbad valley.

For the first few metres I can ease in gently, but soon it gets steep again, with gradients of around 16, 20 per cent. My pulse shoots up to over 150, 160 too... And the weather gets worse with every metre. A damp chill seeps through every seam.

After 45.7 kilometres I call it a day and pitch my tent at the beautifully situated "Betlém" campsite, right on the lakeshore. Out of nowhere the German earworm "Haus am See" ("house by the lake") pops into my head and I collapse onto my sleeping mat, completely exhausted and happy. While I enjoy my dinner of peanuts, pretzel sticks and sickly-sweet isotonic drink, my favourite bird is singing in the next tree. How lovely! I missed that in the "Axel".

My comatose recovery sleep is abruptly interrupted by loud thumping bass. A group of teenagers a few tents along are spending the long weekend here - with schnapps, beer, loud music, chainsaw-sawing contests, nail-hammering etc.

I keep dozing, count the checks on my tent ceiling (lots!) and try to guess the next hit from the jukebox, when suddenly the whole tent lights up strangely and takes on a cosmic colour. And all of a sudden I'm unusually hot. I'm thinking "now your meditation exercises are going a bit too far!" when I realise that the sun has just crept out from behind the clouds!

I quickly jump up and enjoy the warming evening sun with the calm lake at my feet. I can practically feel my inner battery recharging and my weary bones soaking up the warmth. Everything else blurs in that moment, and shortly afterwards I fall into a deep sleep.

Tomorrow I'm off with fresh energy towards Plzeň.
