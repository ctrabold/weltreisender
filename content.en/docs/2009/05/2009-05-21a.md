## Thursday, 21 May 2009

## Once more, slowly

As this morning's post was a bit on the short side, here are a few key facts in peace and quiet:
I set off on Monday morning, after spending the whole weekend packing, optimising and organising.

Figuratively speaking: my panniers now have a YSlow rating of "A". Or put another way: if I'd set off as planned, I'd have been heading home in frustration by Sunday at the latest.

It took quite a bit of willpower to postpone my departure once again, but it was worth it.

Now, after a scorching ride, I'm sitting at the "[Mainblick](http://www.camping-mainblick.de/)" campsite, with my first "Schäufele" (= Franconian roast pork shoulder, i.e. MEAT!) inside me, enjoying the gentle waves of the approaching food coma.

At just under 40 km, today's ride was deliberately a bit shorter:

1. too many people on the cycle path. Today alone I saw two accidents.
2. brain dried out to the size of a prune. Despite regular stops at beer gardens (shandies), it was simply too hot and too crowded...
3. a slight twinge in my left knee. I've been very careful about that since Budapest.

On the plus side, the campsite is excellent and I can finally write to you in peace!

So much happens, every bend brings a different view and different thoughts, that it's really hard to "save" it all up until the evening. Some kind of bike Twitter might come in handy ;)

There's often a lot of situational comedy. Sometimes it's just a bit of graffiti, or the bickering couple coming towards me who whizz past with stony faces. Otherwise there are loads of friendly cyclists and walkers about (though I'm bringing the average age down considerably).

![](/images/2009/05/19/3573041388.jpg)

Tomorrow I'm aiming for Bamberg and I'm looking forward to the night in Kleinholland. Hope you're all well!
