## Thursday, 3 September 2009

## Jalal-Abad - Where's That, Then?

![](/images/2009/09/04/3909446602.jpg)

Just so you know: I'm in [this Jalal-Abad](http://maps.google.de/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=de&geocode=&q=Jalalabad+kirgistan&sll=49.901945,8.852031&sspn=0.007519,0.016501&ie=UTF8&z=8&iwloc=A) and **not** in Afghanistan!

After yesterday's voyage of discovery I've decided to stay here another day and soak up the oriental atmosphere. According to a local, Osh is supposed to be even more awful... others say the opposite. So I'd better see for myself.

The accommodation is very pleasant and super central. There are lots of internet cafés, and I was able to get fresh cash too (only with a VISA card! Cash machines are hard to find).

Yesterday I went to a real bazaar for the first time. It's really incredibly exciting and impressive! I almost bought a freshly skinned ox head - sadly I didn't have a suitable bag with me :)

I also found _the bike corner_ there. It's on the right, directly on Leninskaya. You can't miss it.
