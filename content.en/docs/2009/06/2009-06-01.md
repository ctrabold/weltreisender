## Monday, 1 June 2009

## Spa, Wi-Fi and What a Beer

![](/images/2009/06/01/3609847991.jpg)

From the Betlém campsite I set off into a sunny day towards Plzeň.

Winding country lanes zigzag me through the countryside outside Plzeň. On the map, the E49 main road would be a more direct option. But there's no hard shoulder and lots of lorries use it, so it's too dangerous for my liking.

Instead I enjoy riding through sleepy villages, lovely cornflower fields and wonderfully fragrant forests. All with very, very little traffic and pleasantly mild temperatures. My legs are in top form and today even the climbs roll along nicely. A dream of a ride - until I reach the suburbs of Plzeň.

The traffic picks up considerably and the landscape is dominated by tower blocks and shopping centres. Shortly afterwards I reach the very pretty old town, where I have a picnic by a fountain and let myself be blasted by a party stand set up nearby (European elections!).

Here too the dull four-to-the-floor beat rules, and everything (from current hits to classical pieces) seems to have been fed through the dumb-dumb dance machine.

After the break I ride on to Kozel, my destination for the day. I got to know Kozel in Cheb in the form of a mild, spicy dark beer. That alone justifies the visit. Besides, there's a campsite marked on the map.

Unfortunately police cars block my way. There's obviously been a bad accident up ahead. I quickly look for an alternative route and spot a sign for a hotel, which I follow on the spur of the moment.

It's a steep descent (= no turning back!) and, as it turns out, a four-star hotel awaits me in an old farmhouse with its own brewery and free Wi-Fi. All at a fair price.

![](/images/2009/06/01/3609856483.jpg)

After more than 80 kilometres over hill and dale I move into my luxurious room, have a bath and then let the brewers convince me of their skills.

I try a dark, a semi-dark and a wheat beer. All perfect! I'd even say the semi-dark beats the semi-dark export at the Ratskeller in Darmstadt, Germany. The steak is great too, and afterwards I drift off into a calm, restful sleep.
