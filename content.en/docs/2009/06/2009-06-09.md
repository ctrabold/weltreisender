## Tuesday, 9 June 2009

## The Discovery of LANguor - 2% Complete

![](/images/2009/06/09/3631814279.jpg)

The day starts just before 7am with a deep rumble that makes the building vibrate. I think a train is coming through the room and wake up. It's the building site next door, as I find out later.

Around half past eight the young wild ones wake up too, rampage through the building and get the kayaks ready, which they're soon splashing about in on the river. "Youth hostel," I think, "ah, how lovely :)". I actually feel young, fresh and somehow magically transported back to my youth.

I decide to spend another night here in this fountain of youth. My bike is safely locked up, my room is cheap, the restaurant is round the corner. And I can get into town quickly. I swiftly stash my bits and bobs and set off.

Tram 14 trundles me first through Troja, then along the Vltava and finally right through the city centre (Republic Square, museum). Wide-eyed, I peer left and right into the canyons of buildings and get to experience the city all over again - without any stress or legwork.

At the "Karlovo náměstí" stop I interrupt my sightseeing tour, get off and walk up the hill to the church.

On the way I discover an internet café where I finally want to upload my photos. The café is darkened and very busy. The air reminds me of LAN parties back in the day.

I sit here with lots of other geeks, staring at the screen and waiting for the painfully slow connection to finally transfer my data. I practise patience and hope you'll like the pictures.
