## Saturday, 20 June 2009

## Quick Status Update: Arrived in Hukvaldy, Completely Shattered

![](/images/2009/06/20/3660425567.jpg)

The town should be called HULKvaldy, because that's how I feel.

It was a gruelling day of non-stop rain. The very first climb finished me off, and after 10 kilometres I wanted to throw in the towel in Nový Jičín.

There an elderly gentleman spoke to me and took me to a hotel. On the way he told me a fair bit about the town's history - especially during the Third Reich.

In the end I rode on, because the hotel was still closed.

The landscape is changing noticeably. The Carpathians are getting closer - or rather, I'm already in their western foothills.
