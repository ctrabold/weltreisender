## Wednesday, 17 June 2009

## Relaxa and Deep Peace

![](/images/2009/06/17/3637859245.jpg)

The sun is shining! The weather is glorious and parts of the route are so beautiful I can hardly find the words.

As I leave the deeply unattractive town of Blansko, after a few minutes the path leads into a nature reserve. There are numerous cave systems, dark grottoes stare out of the dense forest. Free climbers hang from steep cliffs and the birds are singing their hearts out. The air is pleasantly cool and smells of fresh moss. Quite a contrast to the dusty, surreal-looking towns of Adamov, Blansko and Brno.

In my destination, Sloup, is the "Relaxa" campsite, where I pitch my tent in the middle of the forest and birdsong and spend the night in incredible peace.

The wind whispers gently around the tent. Now and then a quiet snore saws through the air from the neighbouring tent. Nothing else. What harmony. No number of stars could match that.
