## Wednesday, 3 June 2009

## Four-Star Mosquitoes, Glorious Sunshine and Lots of Wind

![](/images/2009/06/03/3610829186.jpg)

Back on the bike at last. The night was short, dominated by a gripping audiobook, a buzzing mosquito and its itchy bites. I find it amazing that this happens in the most expensive place of my trip so far (in the tent I've always managed to keep the little blighters out).

With a decent tailwind I whizz east towards Brno. Over breakfast I learn that the little town of "Nepomuk" is nearby. Childhood memories come flooding back - "Hallo Spencer", the German children's TV show - and I'd love to take a picture of the place. Maybe I'll even bump into Kasimir? Sadly it's not on my route.

I quickly find a numbered cycle route and put my trust in it. The terrain is hilly as usual. Thanks to the rest day, no problem at all.

What bugs me far more today: I'm realising more and more that my map is completely useless for cycling! Most of the places on the cycle signs aren't on my map at all. What it does give you is a good overview of the Czech Republic and Slovakia - in the local language, too - which is why I chose it.

In practice the map turns out to be a flop and nearly ends up in the next bin. At best it lets you roughly aim for the bigger towns, which is more confusing than helpful given how differently the cycle routes are linked up. So I rely on compass and instinct, which turns out to be far more accurate.

It's a really varied ride: through rolling fields with sweeping views - I even think I caught a glimpse of the top of the "Steffl", St Stephen's Cathedral in Vienna ;) - through deep forests with river crossings and muddy tracks.

The adrenaline ramp "lorry main road" is back too: 16-20% gradient - I'm practically glued to the spot with my fully loaded bike. Instead of a hard shoulder, this time it drops straight into the ditch, and even the draught from the oncoming lorries is so strong that I have to correct carefully with the handlebars. Luckily only cars overtake me.

Just so you know I'm not doing this for fun: there really is a cycle route number on this road.

![](/images/2009/06/03/3610849576.jpg)

That effort has cost quite a bit of energy and I'm slowly longing for the finish. According to plan and gut feeling I should be arriving shortly, when I pass a signpost: "Nepomuk 2km".

How could that happen? I struggle to keep it together. Completely out of sugar and with heavy legs, I picture myself having ridden in circles the whole time. I'd love the ground to swallow me up - or better still: ride straight home...

On my wonderful map there's a campsite marked near Nepomuk. I pluck up my courage and head for Nepomuk after all. Once there, after a few minutes I realise that none of the surrounding places are on the map. Not near the Nepomuk that's on the map, anyway. It dawns on me: Nepomuk has a twin brother. I'm on the right track and can carry on.

After 93 kilometres I reach the "Panorama" hotel in Solenice. After this mountain stage I award myself the polka-dot jersey and fall into bed like a stone. All is well, Galaktika.
