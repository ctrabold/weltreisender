## Friday, 5 June 2009

## Did Someone Order This for Me?

My deep sleep is interrupted around 7pm by a deep bassline. Soundcheck for the upcoming beach party. I can't help laughing and sit up.

Earlier the warden had mentioned that "there'll be music up there" tonight. When picking my pitch I didn't pay attention to such minor details. Now my tent is a few metres behind the trailer on which a rather impressive sound system is enthroned.

There's apparently a sports day on here today. The campsite is right on the reservoir and offers plenty of chances to get sporty. For orientation: this morning I rode through the very dam responsible for this lake, valley and all. Quite a nice "picture", I think. Up here life is in full swing and everyone's having a riot. And down in the valley...

After the soundcheck it's quiet again for a while and I doze on. The music selection certainly promises great things!

Around 8pm things kick off properly: the ground shakes and I'm right "IN DA CLUB"!! It's seriously loud, and the DJ (!) serves up the best of the 70s, 80s, 90s, fairground dodgems and today. After half an hour there's the prize-giving (?) and a couple of presenters get the crowd cheering with what are apparently cracking knee-slappers. At least that's my interpretation.

Then the music carries on with everyone in high spirits. The DJ lets (almost) every track play right through. Only when there are loud complaints (as we'd say back home: "mach mal hädda!" - turn it up!) does he gently fade out and announce the next record - complete with microphone feedback.

Before long the party is in full swing. Nodding along, I do a few crosswords and hope that "no idiot pees on my tent!". Nothing of the sort happens. Instead, at half past ten there's a loud bang and a magnificent firework display crackles high up in the sky - right above me. I have a clear view and can hardly believe it.

![](/images/2009/06/04/3610881394.jpg)

Thank you for this lovely present! With a grin I turn my back on the party crowd and enjoy a few more gems from the Czech record box. What's special about the music selection: almost every song is sung with Czech lyrics. The highlight, I think, is "Cheri Cheri Lady" in Czech. Brilliant!

At four o'clock it's over and everyone goes home in an orderly fashion.

What a day. Quite literally a ride of ups and downs, with everything that goes with it.
