## Tuesday, 2 June 2009

## A Dog's Life

Before a trip you worry about all sorts of dangers.

My top 3 so far looked like this:

1. Cars
2. Dogs
3. Germs in the water / illness

After two weeks in the saddle, the list now looks like this:

1. Small dogs
2. Big dogs
3. Cars

Back in Lower Franconia a mutt almost had a go at my forearm.

I'm riding - suspecting nothing - through a little village and see from a safe distance a long-haired black dog squatting to do its business. At that very moment it spots me, charges at me and, luckily for it, gets yelled back by its owner.

Same thing today on a bend in the Czech Republic: out of nowhere a small, short-haired brown dog races towards me. Only a very loud shout from its owner made the beast turn round.

The regular - extremely loud - barking attacks that make me jump when I ride past front gardens and the like are almost normal by now.

I hate to think what would happen if the animals were running around loose (the countries where that's much more common are still ahead of me).

It's really scary how fixated the animals are on the bike.

## Of course I'm thinking about what to do if it gets serious... Any tips for me?

My first plan at the moment: stop, maybe grab the pump/a stone/my water bottle and shout back.

Could someone find out why (some) dogs react so aggressively to cyclists?

Are there dog psychologists, or maybe Supernanny knows?

## Update from 11/06/2009 after further observation

Thanks for all your tips and ideas! So far there have been no incidents at all.

On the contrary: there have even been some funny moments with dogs. Around here they mostly seem very well behaved and loyal.

- There are a few dogs that bark anyway. No matter who or what, on a bike or not, they bark. Usually very small dogs or guard dogs trained for the job.
- Some dogs watch me go by, curious and alert, but stay quiet.
- And there are lots of dogs that couldn't care less what goes past them. I'm curious to see how things develop and will keep you posted on which method works best.
