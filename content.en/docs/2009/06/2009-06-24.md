## Wednesday, 24 June 2009

## From CZ to PL Was Great

![](/images/2009/06/24/3665005046.jpg)

I've arrived safely in Poland. It's just under 200 kilometres to Kraków.

Shortly before the border I treated myself - partly because of the weather - to two days' rest in a lovely, cheap guesthouse.

Then it was over the hills into Poland.

The change was noticeable. After a fast descent came the border, and I whooshed into Poland at full speed. The road signs change, the cycle route has a different name and suddenly everyone is speaking _polski_.

![](/images/2009/06/24/3661363814.jpg)

After a few strenuous climbs came a looong queue of cars. There's a 14-minute wait at the roadworks traffic lights. I carefully ride past the queue and enjoy the quiet descent to Wisła, where I first refuel with a decent amount of meat.

At a pretty inn by the river there's a proper barbecue going! A litre later I roll on contentedly. The fat sausage and half a roast chicken give me strength.

The route follows the river directly and is lovely and flat. I soon find a campsite, where I'm given a warm welcome.

The mood is exuberant and very family-like. A short while later everyone from the campsite has gathered under the arbour, drinking vodka and listening intently to my travel plans. A young woman from Hürth-Efferen near Cologne, Germany, translates.

The rain drums on the arbour and lightning lights up the evening sky. It's incredibly romantic. Good friends of the campsite owners come from Gundernhausen near Dieburg, so the ice is quickly broken. My photo album from home is passed round enthusiastically.

![](/images/2009/06/24/3664205275.jpg)

Later the barbecue is fired up again and a little midnight snack is served. The shashlik skewer is superb! With it comes fresh grilled garlic. Tip for your next barbecue!!

![](/images/2009/06/24/3665011016.jpg)

A wonderful evening comes to an end, and I feel "warmly welcome" in the truest sense of the word!
