## Thursday, 4 June 2009

## Rock Bottom in the Reservoir Valley

![](/images/2009/06/04/3610877338.jpg)

Yesterday's ride takes its toll. Despite a quiet night and a good breakfast, nothing works at first today. My legs shut down at the first effort. And I haven't the foggiest how I'm ever going to get out of this (deeply dismal) valley.

Apart from a "non-stop dancing" bar (brothel), a gambling den disguised as a hotel and countless abandoned buildings, there's nothing here. What must this place have been like in better times? A shiver runs through me and I feel trapped.

I do what I've always done in moments like this: I find a sunny spot as sheltered from the wind as possible, park the bike safely, have a drink and gaze around. I sit there for over an hour, watching the cars, buses and vans whizz past, and wait.

Is my head empty? Full? No idea. My thoughts mainly circle around: how on earth did I end up here? How do I get out again? Where's the road I'm supposed to follow? How do I get to Prague? Do I even want to go there?

I can feel I'm fighting a proper inner battle: it has a lot to do with Prague and with the fact that I've been trying like mad to get there the whole time. On the other hand I'm almost too far away already. Some magical bond seems to tie me to this city. I decide to stop taking it into account and to keep heading for Brno, which I've intuitively been doing all along anyway.

Something jolts inside me. I snap a photo of myself and Rondolf, my bike, and take the road that suddenly lies clearly ahead of me again.

Slowly and steadily I tackle the climb, which doesn't even hurt any more, and soon find my way out of the valley on a quiet road. In low gears I grind out kilometre after kilometre - stubbornly at my own pace - and break through the first, second and third pain barriers until something like a runner's high sets in.

My fat burning is in full swing and it's actually fun again. Still, I'm glad when I spot the sign for the campsite, where, completely shattered, I'm fast asleep within seconds.
