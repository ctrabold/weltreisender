## Wednesday, 10 June 2009

## A Sluggish Farewell to a Big City, or: No Plan Is a Plan Too

![](/images/2009/06/10/3631822689.jpg)

The alarm goes off on time and I start the day well rested.

After yesterday's map study, my plan is to get onto "EuroVelo" route 4 today and follow it all the way to Kraków (via Brno). According to a graphic I found online yesterday, that means riding a little way back out of Prague. But on which cycle path?

At the tourist information centre in town I get a stack of material on official cycle routes in the Czech Republic. Just not mine.

Slightly crestfallen, I head for the next restaurant with Wi-Fi to do some more research. Nothing to be found.

So I make a virtue of necessity and decide to look for the Elbe (Labe) cycle route.

I've got plenty of material on that one (including route numbers). And I've always wanted to ride the Elbe cycle route anyway.

After another enlightening ride through Prague, I head towards the Elbe. I think I ride the same route through the city five times and keep seeing something new!

The ride out of eastern Prague, though, is pure horror. As tough as chewing gum, badly signposted and anything but scenic.

The industrial estate, infested with mega shopping centres, drags on kilometre after kilometre. When I turn into a side street and switch to a minor road, it's as if the city finally spits me out.

![](/images/2009/06/10/3631822747.jpg)

The switch from dust and noise to sudden idyll is so intense that I have to swallow for a moment.

As if nothing had happened, I now pedal through the approaching Elbe valley with glorious views, surrounded by birdsong and fresh, open air.

The route along the Elbe is lovely. Above all, lovely and flat. ;)

![](/images/2009/06/10/3631829217.jpg)

In "Lysá nad Labem" I find a pretty guesthouse, with an even prettier bar round the corner, where an even, even prettier waitress serves me delicious Kofola and Kozel pivo.

I'm curious to see how the journey continues and raise a toast to the Elbe, Rhine, Main, Isar, Danube, Spree, Pearl River and of course the Gersprenz back home in Dieburg!
