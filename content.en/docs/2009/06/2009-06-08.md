## Monday, 8 June 2009

## There Are No Shortcuts … and No Detours Either

![](/images/2009/06/08/3610128951.jpg)

The day starts with packing and breakfast. I'm looking forward to being back on the official cycle route later.

The hills at the start of the route give me a bit of a stomach ache, though. Picture it: I very nearly filled the potholes with my muesli. In the end it stays at the full-bodied taste of Nutella, choco pops and orange juice. OJ in the morning = big mistake, but of course I thought I knew better ;)

Yesterday I set today's plan for the day (!) as follows:

- Conquer the hills
- Ride back to the place where I lost the trail
- Get money from the bank
- Find the signposts and roll happily along the last stretch to Prague.

Item 1 is ticked off successfully. But by the second one I already think I'm smarter than my plan: just as I reach the town, I see the cycle route sign. Great! That was quick. So I was more or less on the right track and just need to turn left instead of right.

I turn round on the spot. A few kilometres later, no cycle sign again. This can't be true, I curse (passing drivers probably think Rumpelstiltskin himself is standing by the roadside, having lost another bet).

After another U-turn I ride further back this time, to the market square/savings bank (item 2!) and get money (item 3!!).

Then I calmly ride the route once more and suddenly see **THE SIGN!** It's hanging exactly above the spot I'd suspected. Impossible to miss. How could that happen? I remember: yesterday I'd waved a car past here and even took a short break under the sign. After that, one thing led to another and I got lost.

Never mind: from now on everything runs like clockwork. The route is excellent and Prague is getting closer fast.

So I can tick off the last item of the day to my complete satisfaction. Just after three I cross the Prague city limits and then ride leisurely through the suburbs and a lovely city forest.

![](/images/2009/06/08/3610911276.jpg)

Then it's many kilometres along the banks of the Vltava. The clouds over Prague clear and the sun comes out! The riverside promenade is full of cyclists, joggers and inline skaters. Very relaxed!

![](/images/2009/06/08/3610124771.jpg)

In these few hours I get more "Ahojs", "Hellos" and friendly looks than on the entire ride so far. The atmosphere is impressive. So are the buildings I saw on my little tour of the city.

In the north of Prague (near Troja) I take a room in a youth hostel right on the river. There's a training course for kayakers here and it's a meeting point for cyclists, inline skaters and so on. A sporty beer-garden vibe in the best of weather.

After some tasty gnocchi and five "Kofolas" (the Czech version of cola) I'm now ready for bed. Tomorrow I'm heading into town.
