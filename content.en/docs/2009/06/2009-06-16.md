## Tuesday, 16 June 2009

## Brno Was a Bummer

Dear all, I'm feeling crushed.

![](/images/2009/06/16/3637836323.jpg)

The ride to Brno was lovely, but even the (long-awaited) arrival in the city felt like someone had punched me in the stomach. Scorching sun mixed with chip fat and dodgems, then what felt like forever along the busy road to the centre.

When I get to the city centre, it hits me for good. It's even hotter, extremely stuffy and packed to bursting. On top of that my blood sugar is through the floor and all the blood suddenly drains into my legs.

White as a sheet, I shuffle - zombie-like - through the narrow lanes looking for somewhere to sleep.

To my horror I discover there are only four-star hotels for miles around. No guesthouse, no campsite. Prices start at 2000 Kč (almost 100 EUR).

In desperation I flee - in a kind of displacement activity - into a map shop and buy cycling maps from Kraków to Ukraine... But even after that I still have nowhere to stay.

Riding on is out of the question, so I walk into one of the nearest establishments and let the condescending looks bounce off me. It's actually the first time I've been "welcomed" with a disparaging look.

Friendliness is in very short supply here. I don't spot any during my entire stay in this city.

The hotel I end up checking into is a mockery that I still haven't quite got over today.

I pay 2090 Kč for a shabby room - with a direct view of the building site opposite, which, incidentally, wakes me up promptly just after 7. When I want to shower, I'm greeted first by dark brown water. Same when brushing my teeth.

The bed is even mustier than the room, and I can easily picture secret police informers and their sweethearts once meeting here for a rendezvous.

My plan to avenge this cheek at breakfast at the latest has evidently been seen through by the hotel management: with the modest buffet, the staff offer neither a reply to my "Dobrý den!" (good morning), nor clean cutlery, nor any other sign of human emotion.

All in all, a rather sobering affair.

After 15 kilometres I leave Brno on the excellently built Greenways route "KRAKOW-MORAVIA-VIENNA" and ride leisurely towards Blansko.

Today's destination is _Sloup_, where there's a campsite marked on the map.
