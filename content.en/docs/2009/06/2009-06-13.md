## Saturday, 13 June 2009

## Fast as Can Be with a Tailwind for Me

![](/images/2009/06/13/3632738672.jpg)

With big strides I head towards Brno, which is still about two days away.

The landscape is getting hillier and hillier. The highlight is a closed-off section through incredibly quiet forest. The loudest things are my breathing and the crunch of gravel under my tyres. Otherwise, pure woodland idyll.

When I stop for a short break, a strange bird flies out of the bushes: it's big, stork-like with long legs and has a bright red beak. Its red belly is framed by black feathers. Since I don't have my bird guide to hand, I spontaneously christen it the "clown stork". Does anyone know more?

I reach the "Borova" campsite on time and pitch my tent not far from a pretty little babbling brook. What a harmonious day - until suddenly a crude death-metal double-bass-drum inferno erupts across the site. A gang of teenagers has made itself at home on the opposite side. Luckily there are a few little cabins in between. Even so, it's extremely loud and the music is not funny at all.

As I walk back to the tent after brushing my teeth, I notice a little fire crackling outside every cabin. I can do that too! A few stray branches are quickly found and my little hobo stove is set up in no time.

After just a few minutes a lovely fire is crackling outside my door too.

It goes on until late into the night and is hellishly good fun. Under a starry sky I let my thoughts circle around the flames and gather more wood until there's none left to find. So I've passed my trial by fire as well. ;)

The noise in the distance dies down and I retreat contentedly into my sleeping bag.
