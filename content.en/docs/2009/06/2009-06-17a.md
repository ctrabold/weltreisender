## Wednesday, 17 June 2009

## A Blue Surprise Off the Beaten Track

![](/images/2009/06/16/3638642964.jpg)

Fully rested, I start the day by riding to the shop in the village centre for breakfast. Then I ride to a lovely spot in front of a cave and enjoy the fresh rolls.

After that I follow the perfectly built cycle path towards Prostějov. Unfortunately the cycle path turns out to be a decoy and quite suddenly mutates into a hiking trail. I've gone the wrong way.

Thanks to the map I find my position and realise I'm on the "blue-white" hiking trail. Turning back is out of the question, so I follow the ever-narrowing path.

The "path" has everything a proper hiking trail needs. Steep slopes, river crossings, fallen trees. Several times I have to take off the panniers and lift Rondolf over thick tree trunks. Once I get stuck in a deep mud hole and need a good five minutes to free myself.

In those minutes I'm the happiest man on earth. Caked in mud, I return to the bright farm track after hours and take a break, watching swallows tending their nests. Thanks to my new binoculars I can follow the show up close.

A few metres further on, the hiking trail crosses the official cycle route again and I wistfully turn off. That was absolutely glorious!

Shortly afterwards, lorries and sports cars are racing past me again. As compensation, there's the longest descent of my trip so far. It's downhill for more than half an hour.

I quickly abandon my ambitious plan to beat my top speed so far. There are too many potholes on the road and the descent needs all my concentration.

A little later I reach the campsite near Prostějov and pitch my tent under the watchful eye of Czech youth. A school class is apparently camping here and the night is going to be loud again.
