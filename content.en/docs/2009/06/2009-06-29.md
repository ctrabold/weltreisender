## Monday, 29 June 2009

Arrival in Kraków

Scrambled eggs in the morning chase your troubles away. Add some bacon and a pretty garnish, and all the worries of the previous day and the night are forgotten.

Well fortified, I ride back to the Greenway, which today should really be called the "Highway", because after a few metres it goes up so steeply that Rondolf refuses and I have to push.

After the rain the path is unrideable. The back wheel keeps spinning. Even pushing sends my pulse to the max. At first the path is tarmac and heavily covered in moss, so my feet keep slipping away. Getting back in the saddle is impossible.

"Walking on tiptoe" turns out to be the best tactic, and so I slowly scramble up the hill, panting.

After this magnificent start, the rest of the route - apart from one or two canine intermezzos - is quiet and runs through beautiful dense forest.

As I'm pedalling along, a family of elk crosses the path in front of me. At first I think a horse is coming out of the undergrowth. But then I recognise the peculiar lanky gait. Then comes the young one, then the bigger father. A wonderful feeling to see these animals in the wild. Until now I'd only known them "with enclosure".

At 5pm I reach Kraków. At a little roadside stall I stock up on fruit, including a quarter of a watermelon and a carrot, and ride into the nearest park to celebrate the first proper sunny day in ages!

Thanks to my Opinel knife it turns into a real feast, and I relish (!) every bite - and every drop of sweat running down my forehead as I eat. Sunshine at last!

I check into the "Daisy" hotel and let the restless night pass. The room is smaller than my tent. Claustrophobia sets in. On top of that, doors slam in the corridor until just after one.

At some point I fall asleep with music in my ears. My last thought: tomorrow it's straight into town. No breakfast. Get out of here fast.
