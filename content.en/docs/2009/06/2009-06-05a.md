## Friday, 5 June 2009

Over Gentle Hills towards Tábor

I catch up on the night with a long lie-in until just after 11. Pitching and striking the tent and packing are becoming quite routine, so just before 12 I'm off towards Mělišov. First I have to ride five kilometres from the campsite back to my original route.

My legs are much more relaxed than yesterday and my morale is fully back too. And every now and then the sun even peeps out from behind the thick cloud cover.

At the pit stop in Mělišov I pick up fresh cash, loads of cycling maps and two handy German-Czech dictionaries (a straight dictionary and a collection of practical everyday phrases). Suddenly the world feels within reach again. The feeling of striding through the world with both feet on the ground is back.

The rest of the day flies by. I quickly find the cycle path that leads me out of town and catapults me towards Tábor.

So, just before seven, I reach Tábor's pretty old town bathed in the loveliest evening light and quickly find a cheap guesthouse near the smart market square.

After a potato-heavy dinner I go on the prowl for Wi-Fi. I strike lucky at the friendly "Cafe MP7" and enjoy a nightcap to good music.

With _Phil Collins_ in my ears, I send lots of love back home!
