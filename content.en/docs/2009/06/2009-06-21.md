## Sunday, 21 June 2009

## Music sounds better with you

![](/images/2009/06/22/3660514733.jpg)

Today I rode with earphones in for the first time. A strange feeling, watching the unfamiliar landscape rush past with your favourite songs in your ears.

During "The Riddle" I whistled along happily and forgot about the rain clouds filling the sky from edge to edge. As I listened to Bono's voice, a wild dog suddenly leapt after me (getting off really does help!!). During "Ding!" by Seeed I found my way back to the path in the forest. And Luciano made the arrival in dreary _Třinec_ bearable.

Still, I prefer riding without music. The surroundings feel more intense when birdsong and bleating sheep reach your brain unfiltered.

The descents especially demand maximum concentration. Since the roads are barely wider than a car, I often have to stop for oncoming traffic.

When I have to ride behind a horse-drawn cart for ages because the road is too narrow to overtake, I don't even want to overtake. It's so lovely and harmonious and relaxing behind the cart. The smell of horses, the clip-clop and a gentle downhill pamper legs and soul. With a heavy heart I turn off at the next junction and follow the cycle path into town.

The climbs are getting noticeably steeper and more frequent. Then the music is often more of a distraction than a source of strength.

There's a new uphill record: I didn't look at the clock exactly, but it felt like hours at over 16%. The longest and toughest climb so far.

After 72.59 kilometres and 5:14 hours on the hilly track, I peel off my damp things at a hotel in the little town of _Třinec_. They just won't dry, and the weather forecast promises more rain and cold, if anything.

Time for "Don't Worry, Be Happy" - I'm riding on tomorrow anyway and visiting our neighbour Poland for the first time in my life. Really looking forward to it!
