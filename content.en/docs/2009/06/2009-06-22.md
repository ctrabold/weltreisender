## Monday, 22 June 2009

## TV with Wanderlust - Rest Day, Rain and Chilling Out

![](/images/2009/06/24/3661363814.jpg)

From the morning on, raindrops pelt against the window of my room on the 9th floor of the "Steel" hotel. I don't even want to open the curtain.

Outside it's grey and wet. Plus the industrial idyll of Třinec.

I optimise my panniers, cut out unnecessary bits of map and throw two books overboard.

Whistling, I ride through the rain. It seems the cold and the rain don't bother me. But on the very first descent the water presses against my skin and there's an unpleasant chill on my upper body. My rain trousers are stuck to my legs.

After 20 kilometres, that's it for the day. I take the first opportunity and check into the "Bela" guesthouse. After a hearty dinner I fall into a deep sleep, interrupted only the next morning by a brief breakfast.

The rest of the time I lie in bed, tune out the splashing in the gutter and watch TV. A thousand thoughts whirl through my head. Sometimes racing, sometimes agonisingly slow.

Tomorrow it's on into the unknown. Somehow I'm glad to be leaving the Czech Republic. Most people here are only moderately obliging. A "Bon appétit" or "Good morning!" is a real rarity (I can count them on one hand). Somehow I feel strangely rushed here. I'm hoping for more friendliness in Poland.
