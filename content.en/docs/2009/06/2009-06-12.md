## Friday, 12 June 2009

## Farewell, Elbe

![](/images/2009/06/12/3632689974.jpg)

At five o'clock I'm woken by the rain. It's pelting loudly against my window. When the alarm goes off, the sun is shining.

As I set off, the clouds gather again and I start out in full rain gear.

After a few kilometres the expected cloudburst arrives, and this time there's a strong crosswind. It almost blows Rondolf and me into the field. Hard graft for the arms.

It's raining heavily, but nothing like yesterday.

![](/images/2009/06/12/3632689994.jpg)

The road to Pardubice drags on uneventfully. Only the weather is annoying, because it's so changeable. No sooner have I stowed the rain jacket than it starts raining. If I air the sauna under my helmet, the wind picks up again. It goes on like this all day. It saps your strength, and I'm glad when I reach the nondescript town.

![](/images/2009/06/12/3632683668.jpg)

The hotel I check into with a sinking feeling reminds me of the Prisma (80s!) in its décor, and the bed squeaks. I pray I'll be the only guest tonight ;)

In desperation I watch TV. There's the film about the "Huber Buam" (the free-climbing brothers), a travel documentary about the Forest Carpathians and one about Vietnam.

The overpriced hotel is forgotten and I fall asleep feeling I'm on the right path.
