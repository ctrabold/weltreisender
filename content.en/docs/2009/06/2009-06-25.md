## Thursday, 25 June 2009

## Pinpoint Landing in Bielsko-Biała

![](/images/2009/06/25/3664213075.jpg)

Man, am I hungover today! I haven't had that much vodka and beer in me for ages. But it was great.

After saying goodbye to everyone, I still set off on time and, luckily, ride along the Wisła to Skoczów on beautifully flat terrain. There I lose the Greenways route (again) and carry on under my own steam (again) on busy country roads in extremely poor condition.

The bumps are especially treacherous, because you only feel them when you ride over them (jumpstyle!). You can't make them out from a distance. Cycle path? Not a chance! With this much traffic it's extremely dangerous and no fun at all.

On top of that, since crossing the Polish border, the respect (= distance) shown to cyclists has noticeably decreased. The number of black clouds of soot, on the other hand, has increased considerably.

Irritated and downcast, I reach Bielsko-Biała, where after 50 kilometres and 3:44 hours I find a room at the [Hotel "President"](http://www.hotelpresident.pl).

As I carry the panniers from the bike into the hotel, a thunderstorm crashes down, cleaning the dusty air within seconds and finally cooling the muggy air.

Good thing I can spread my things out in a dry room.
