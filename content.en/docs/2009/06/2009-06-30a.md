## Tuesday, 30 June 2009

On New Paths in Kraków

Despite the summer weather, there's a thunderstorm in my head today.

The restless night is in my bones. And the unfriendly manner of the hotel staff doesn't exactly make for a cheerful start to the day. What worries me most, though, is my schedule.

Someone - probably me ;) - put 23 July as the start date of the Russian visa. That's going to be very tight, considering what's still ahead of me. In my head I go through possible solutions, but don't find a clear one.

So I ride into Kraków with a heavy heart and barely take in the beautiful city panorama unfolding in front of me. The Wisła winds harmoniously towards Kraków and the cycle path is wide and quiet. Above me the sun shines in a blue sky. The city spreads out before me like a postcard.

A few metres further on, the Greenway from Vienna to Kraków ends and the next one begins: the Amber Trail. From Kraków to Budapest. I'll follow it over the Tatra Mountains into Slovakia. But first, it's time to recover.

Shortly afterwards I check into a hotel in the centre and enjoy a big chocolate milkshake on the market square.

In that moment I really arrive in Kraków. A very beautiful city and a great atmosphere. I feel completely at ease.

In the distance the famous trumpeter sounds, and I'm sure everything will work out.
