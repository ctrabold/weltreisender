## Tuesday, 2 June 2009

## Rest Day (hopefully makes tired muscles strong again)

Today I prescribe myself a rest day, listen to an audiobook ("Ausgebrannt", a German thriller) and let cabin fever set in.

After a fairly sunny morning it suddenly goes dark outside and fat raindrops pelt against my window. I roll over and give the narrator my full attention again. When I sit up again, the sun is back out.

To round off the day I do a little loop around the block. Without luggage it feels like riding on chewing gum.

In the distance I can see a band of rain coming down. It's approaching fast. The wind picks up and I have to hurry so I don't get soaked after all.

That really was the most exciting thing that happened today. Tomorrow I want to move on - whatever the weather. End this standstill and breathe in the wide open spaces again.
