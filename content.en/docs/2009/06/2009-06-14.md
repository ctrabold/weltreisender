## Sunday, 14 June 2009

Pasta with Sauce from the Hallway

The little hobo stove has burnt everything cleanly. There's hardly any ash, and after two taps the frame is neatly stowed away again.

Just after 10 I set off. After 850 metres it's breakfast of pivo and a "Picanti" pizza in a sunny forest hut. Yes! Sunny!! It's boiling hot and my body is crying out for fuel.

After that it's a slow slog through the hills. I keep stopping ("There's always room for a Kofola!") and take the day very easy.

When, thirsty, I open my second water bottle, I'm horrified to notice a putrid smell.

Until now I'd only ever topped up the water and never changed it. Combined with the isotonic powder and the heat, that makes for undrinkable water. With a heavy heart I tip the useless ballast away.

After another break my reserves are topped up again and, as they say in Cologne, "et läuft!" - it's rolling!

I soon reach Jimramov. Near Dalečín comes the surprise of the day: the road is closed to motor vehicles by a barrier and curves beautifully along the shore of the reservoir. On the climbs that follow I show the Czechs what's what and practically sprint up the hill. The sugar from the Kofolas is now exactly where it should be, and it's simply glorious to pant my lungs out with that panorama.

After a rather lengthy search for a place to sleep, there's tasty food with an amusing "German" menu (see headline - a garbled attempt at "pasta with sauce of the house").

How a day can turn around, I think, and fall asleep exhausted.
