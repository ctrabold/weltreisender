## Tuesday, 9 June 2009

## Internet Addiction Found and Prague Experienced

The time in the internet café flew by! I finally read the most important news of the last few weeks from the newsgroups, the blogs I subscribe to and my Twitter channels - no, what am I saying: soaked it all up! Luckily the computers had Windows installed. So I could FINALLY soothe my intense craving for a computer and recharge my geek batteries.

Unfortunately that meant I saw nothing of the city - except on the way back, which I had to walk because I'd left all my money at the internet café and didn't have enough for the tram.

I got the necessary pictures, info and impressions of Prague from prag-kompakt.cz and stayed dry into the bargain, because outside it was bucketing down like mad again. Geeks just live smarter. :)

Ha! Fooled you!!

Here's what really happened: I was massively relieved to emerge from that breeding ground. Once my eyes had got used to daylight again, I went off exploring.

Outside the sun shone all over Prague (I just love that pun - in German "den ganzen Prag" sounds like "the whole day"). So in my little hiking boots I got to discover a wonderful city and stretch my legs at the same time. The footwear was spot on. It's pretty hilly here.

Despite the countless tourists, the atmosphere is very pleasant. There's all sorts to see. Even old culture-dodger Christión gets carried away.

For dinner just now I treated myself - in full view of all the other guests - to two plates of pasta. The two plates came at the same time. One after the other might have been smarter. I managed it anyway.

Now, well fed, it's off to bed, and tomorrow on towards Brno. That'll slim me down again.
