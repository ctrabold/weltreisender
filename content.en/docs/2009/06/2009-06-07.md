## Sunday, 7 June 2009

A Different Kind of BOOM BOOM BOOM

Thanks to the non-stop disco, I've got used to the bass on campsites by now. The banging I got to witness last night - sometimes in 4/4, sometimes in 16/16 time - was another matter.

The _Laguna_ hotel is in an utterly peaceful spot on Lake "Slapy" (sounds like "Schlappsi" - German for a bit of a wimp! :). At reception I learn that I'm the only guest. Perfect conditions for a restful night, then.

When I go to my room just after midnight, I hear loud banging and furniture being shoved around. What's going on now?

It goes on for quite a while and I keep pausing my audiobook, because the commotion is making me uneasy. Every now and then someone shouts something in Czech. Surely there isn't a fire or something? I'm getting restless and try to concentrate on the audiobook _AQUA tm_ by Jean-Marc Ligny.

Shortly afterwards I realise that a passionate night of love has broken out in the room next door and the banging is going right through the building. The bed squeaks, she squeaks, he grunts. Then silence. A few moments later the whole thing starts again. To top it all, things are getting rather steamy in my audiobook too.

The act drags on until 4am and I have a real struggle getting to sleep. It's an "attendance" in the truest sense of the word - the old German word for intercourse, _Beiwohnung_, literally means "being present" (see also [http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beischlaf](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beischlaf)) - and I wake up just before nine feeling like I've been run over.

After breakfast I lie down again _for a moment_ and enjoy a wonderfully quiet sleep. When I wake up it's almost 1pm, and I've made up my mind: today I'm neither getting on the bike nor putting myself through any other kind of sport.

Hoping the couple will be quieter tonight, or even gone, I book another night.

Now I'm off for a walk round the lake to stretch my legs. The sun's out and it's looking like it'll be a nicer day.
