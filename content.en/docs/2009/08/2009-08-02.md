## Sunday, 2 August 2009

## Recovering in Tsimlyansk

![](/images/2009/08/03/3832555557.jpg)

The night is short. Shortly after six the dog wakes me with frantic yapping.

A loud primal scream and a STOI! ("Stop!") drive it off. Tail between its legs, it makes itself scarce.

Fortified like that, the last few kilometres to Volgodonsk fly by. Sadly, the town isn't exactly the kind of place you'd want to linger. The traffic and the noise mean I settle for a quick breakfast by the river.

After that I pedal at an easy pace towards Tsimlyansk, where I do a quick shop and retreat to a gostinitsa. Yesterday's ride has taken its toll and I doze in my room until the evening.

And yet the view here is gorgeous. The lake starts just a few metres from the gostinitsa, and from the cliffs I have a wonderful view far, far out into the blue. I toast the moment with a cold kvass and let my thoughts wander.

Over the last few days a touch of homesickness has crept in every now and then. A longing for things to be easy. To be understood without lots of words. Everything here takes effort, which really gets on my nerves, especially on rest days.

Still, my appetite for new things wins out, and I feel stronger than the grumpy woman behind the counter.

As I drift off, images and moments from my trip come into my head for the first time. Pictures from the Czech Republic, Poland and Slovakia. The scent of Crimea. The sweet, warm headwind in absolute stillness.

Breathing deeply in and out, I fall into a lovely sleep.
