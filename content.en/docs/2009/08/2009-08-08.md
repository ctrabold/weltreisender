## Saturday, 8 August 2009

Volgograd

I've arrived safely in Volgograd!

The ride here was pure horror - I wouldn't wish roads like these on any cyclist in the world! The traffic is brutal and there's no hard shoulder. I often have to ride half in the field. Surprisingly, though, the mayhem around me leaves me fairly calm.

Inside, I'm very tense for other reasons. Images of the madness that took place here keep going round in my head. The landscape is unbelievably vast. Beautiful, but also mentally exhausting.

In Volgograd I find a hotel not far from the memorial. I book two nights straight away, because I'd like to get to know the city better.

In the evening I walk through the park and remember the dead in the golden Hall of Remembrance.

Today I've reached a big goal - one that seems almost insignificantly small against this backdrop.

A phone call home cheers me up a bit. I can feel how incredibly hard it is to squeeze all these impressions down a phone line. I can also hear how hoarse my voice is from all the road dust. High time for a break!

From here I'm planning to head south towards Astrakhan next week, either by train or by boat. I've almost given up on a cycling trip overland. Let's see how things turn out in the end. ;-)

Greetings to you all!
