## Tuesday, 4 August 2009

## Pedal to the Metal, What a Thrill

![](/images/2009/08/04/3833418740.jpg)

Riding along the farm track is enormous fun. Quickly and unexpectedly I reach the tarmac road to Termonskaya. Suddenly the track ends and I carry on along an excellent road.

After two hours, though, my initial euphoria slowly starts to sag. The road goes dead straight and uphill for ever.

The hills are brutal. Not so much because they're steep, but because the climbs are unbelievably long. Just as I've summoned up the courage to ride the last few metres to the crest, I realise that after it the road keeps going up.

![](/images/2009/08/04/3833420710.jpg)

The gradient is so mean that you're constantly convinced you're about to reach the top of the hill, only to find, disappointed, that it just keeps going.

It frays the nerves. On top of that, my water is running low fast. The sun is beating down mercilessly and I can feel my mouth getting drier and drier.

Thirst is a very unpleasant need. Suddenly every lorry seems to be carrying something drinkable, the rivers on the map are - unbelievably - dry (!) and even the melon sellers at the roadside have disappeared.

Luckily I find a river that still has water in it and top up my supply using the filter.

At the magazin (shop) in Loschnoy I refill my drinks supply and, with my pulse at 155, blast the last few kilometres towards Popov. Madness!

After 88.5 kilometres I roll off to the right into a field and pitch my tent. The little clump of trees looks ideal for tonight's accommodation.
