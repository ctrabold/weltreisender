## Thursday, 6 August 2009

## The Road to My Limits

![](/images/2009/08/05/3832642757.jpg)

A hot, dry wind blows in my face all day today. It's uphill all the way. Even when it goes downhill for once, I still have to pedal.

It's very frustrating. Why on earth am I doing this to myself, the thought keeps nagging. What I wouldn't give for a melon right now!

It goes on like this for almost 70 kilometres, until at some point I reach the T-junction with the motorway where - oh joy! - there's even a melon stall.

The honeydew melon is superb and gives me strength for the last stretch. At a motel I wash the dust of the last few days off my body.

![](/images/2009/08/06/3833455612.jpg)

To top it all, during my bedtime reading Josef Knecht from Hesse's _The Glass Bead Game_ dies in an ice-cold mountain lake. How sad!

I can hardly sleep for the heat. I've got at least a moderate case of sunstroke and cool myself down with damp cloths.

I toss and turn restlessly, think back to the book and try to ignore my neighbours.

Tomorrow I'm planning to reach Volgograd!
