## Thursday, 3 December 2009

Off We Go

Arrived safely in Emeishan on Monday.

The last few days have been a whirlwind. The change of scene - and above all of temperature - "Hong Kong - Guangzhou - Emeishan" was noticeable every single time.

Today we're heading for Ebian. The next bigger town I'm aiming for is Xichang.

The route goes past the "Dafengding Natural Ecosystem Reserve" to Meigu. I'm hoping for a bit of peace and quiet there, a break from the Chinese hustle and bustle.

The chances are good, because the "white road" on the map (= minor road) already promises less traffic than the overcrowded 213.
