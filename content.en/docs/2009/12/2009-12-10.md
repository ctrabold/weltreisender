## Thursday, 10 December 2009

This is your celebration! - Festive Mood in Kunming

The city greets me with sunshine and spring-like weather. Wow! Off with the fur!

The youth hostel is centrally located and rather likeable, the four-bed room bright and friendly.

I've already got the first visa: Thailand. It was even free. Unfortunately I don't know whether they granted my request for a 60-day visa... we're supposed to ask again on arrival. Exactly what I wanted to avoid.

Today I handed in my application for Myanmar. In a week I'll find out whether I get the visa.

Until then I'll pass the time here, watch the Chinese doing Christmas and nurse my right knee, which has been aching a bit since Xichang. There's even good German beer! Pictures:
On the Christmas front: Christmas music is playing in every shop (it was the same in Hong Kong). There are Christmas trees on every corner, or Father Christmases climbing up the walls.

"This is your celebration!" beams the woman from the hostel as I take in all the goings-on, laughing. Crazy!
