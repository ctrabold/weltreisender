## Tuesday, 15 December 2009

Heading for Laos!

There's wonderful news from Kunming:

Today I received the visa and the official letter of entry for Myanmar! So tomorrow I can carry on south. Hooray!

My planned route goes via Yuanjiang, Jinghong and Mengla down to the border with Laos.

The evening before last I sat over the map of Laos with an Israeli for ages.

He loves the country and has infected me with travel fever - which I'm deliberately going to keep low, though. Fittingly, I've discovered that my clinical thermometer gave up the ghost in the mountain cold. The mercury isn't budging an inch any more, anyway (tip: take a digital thermometer rather than a classic one!).

There's nothing keeping me in Kunming any more. The Christmas madness is almost unbearable. Everything is "over-decorated" with kitschy Christmas tat, and if anything looks daft, it's little Chinese people in bright red dangly Father Christmas hats.

The Chinese versions of "Jingle Bells" and "White Christmas" are beautifully, horribly off-key, too!

And of course - after Christmas comes: New Year's Eve! For which people here are already decking themselves out in nice Western style.

How glad I'll be when I can go elephant-spotting in the tropical rainforest! Because that will be possible in the region ahead.

Pictures: [http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xishuangbanna](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xishuangbanna)

If my research is right, Kunming is also the last really big city on my trip. From now on everything gets "a bit smaller" again. The biggest cities - Bangkok and Singapore - have fewer inhabitants than Kunming. Not to mention the towns in Laos. Let's see how I get on there ;)

Current state of planning for the cycling route:

I'll try to cross Laos from north to south.

## Towns

- Luang Nam Tha
- Luang Prabang
- Viangchan / Vientiane
- Phonsavan
- Pakxe

Near Pakxe I'll cross over into Thailand.

If I still have the time and the inclination, I'll ride across Thailand and up to _Mae Sai_. In _Mae Sai_ there's a border crossing into Myanmar. In Myanmar I'll then take the "4" towards _Inle Lake_.

From there up to Mandalay and then on to Minbu, Prome, Yangon, and then back into Thailand at Mae Sot.

In Thailand I'll head strictly south - maybe with a little detour to Bangkok, if I have the time and fancy a big city (so probably not). Otherwise I'd take the "4" straight down to Malaysia.

In Malaysia I'll probably ride down the "3" along the east coast to the ferry to Singapore. The west coast is supposed to be rather boring. And Kuala Lumpur et al. don't interest me in the slightest.

So much for the current plan. Subject to change. Details about the towns mentioned can be found on Wikipedia.
