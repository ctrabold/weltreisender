## Tuesday, 1 December 2009

Bye bye Guangzhou!

​30 November to 1 December

The days in Guangzhou fly by, and on Monday I fly back to Emeishan with a slight pang of farewell. It was lovely! Thanks, Daniel, for some brilliant days!

Monday is a good example of how easy it is to travel without a bike:

Bus to the airport in the morning, last-minute ticket at the counter, four hours later at the bus station in Chengdu, six hours later back with Rondolf in Emeishan.

Of course that's tiring too - in the evening I felt as if I'd been through the wash - but with the bike something like that is much harder. Just getting to the airport would have been a lot more nerve-racking. Not to mention riding the whole way. Especially at this time of year!!

After this episode of "A Backpacker's Life" I'm glad to be back with my beloved bike and to be pedalling again soon.

After the exciting days in Hong Kong and Guangzhou, Emeishan is downright boring. The drop in temperature from "hot and muggy" to "damp and cold" alone is hitting me hard. Blocked sinuses, headache, a runny nose.

Highlight: I meet up again with Ron, with whom I cycled through the Taklamakan Desert. We're planning to ride on to Kunming together, probably on Thursday.
