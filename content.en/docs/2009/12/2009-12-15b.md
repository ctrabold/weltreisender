## Tuesday, 15 December 2009

New Year's Eve at Cat Mountain

Don't count your chickens before they hatch:

No sooner have I sent off the post about my onward route than an email from Daniel in China lands in my inbox: New Year's Eve plans.

Long story short: the south will have to wait. Tomorrow I'm heading east first - final destination [Guilin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guilin).

At New Year we'll crack open a bottle of bubbly on the Li River (more, depending on the boat's capacity). After the weekend I'll cycle back to Kunming, where I'll pick up my original route again (see previous post).

Cycling circles reckon on 3 weeks to get to Guilin, which is why I'm (once again) checking the train connections.

I'd really like to do most of the route by bike, because in terms of scenery and unspoilt countryside it's supposed to be particularly lovely...

More on that in the New Year ;)
