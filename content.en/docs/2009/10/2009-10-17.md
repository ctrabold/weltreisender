## Saturday, 17 October 2009

## Sign of Life - All OK

I've arrived safely in China and I'm currently in Huatugou - in the middle of nowhere in Xinjiang province.

I'm fine!!

Since Osh I've been travelling with Noel (USA). In Kashi, Ron (Netherlands) joined us.

Next stop is Xining, then Kunming.

Behind me lie indescribable weeks:

![](/images/2009/09/20/4018322617.jpg)

A magnificent farewell from Kyrgyzstan. For days the snow-white peaks of the Pamir mountains follow us on our right. One morning a Kyrgyz shepherd appears out of nowhere, and in the blink of an eye I'm enjoying my first ride on a real Kyrgyz horse. Brilliant!

![](/images/2009/09/20/4018320361.jpg)

![](/images/2009/09/20/4018321317.jpg)

Then miserably long bumpy tracks again until we arrive in the great country of China. The Silk Road is real back-breaking work!

![](/images/2009/09/19/4018309303.jpg)

![](/images/2009/09/19/4018315377.jpg)

![](/images/2009/09/20/4031838736.jpg)

![](/images/2009/09/21/4031855022.jpg)

My first close encounter with China during my week of rest in Kashi, including Chinglish highlights, incredible misunderstandings and real culinary treats.

After that, crossing the world's second-largest desert, the [Taklamakan](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taklamakan), on a good road... sadly with countless ANNOYING interruptions because of bridge works.

![](/images/2009/10/11/img_2096_4019125444_o.jpg)

All under the cloak of the longest communications blackout since the start of my trip.

![](/images/2009/10/15/img_2217_4018956395_o.jpg)

The route was very tough, formative and beautiful. Rondolf is in fine fettle.

My 2-litre Sigg aluminium bottle burst in the night frost. Otherwise everything's great!
