## Wednesday, 22 July 2009

## Danish delicacies

![](/images/2009/07/22/3769444346.jpg)

Completely exhausted, I stop at a mini-market. The route has pushed me to my limits. The short night also means that halfway along I have to take a quick afternoon nap. "Foetal position in the pine forest" style.

All around me there's colourful beach life. There are tents everywhere. Looks like I've stumbled upon a campsite by chance. As I'm wondering whether to ride on, a not entirely unfamiliar couple suddenly pops into my tunnel vision. It's the Danish couple I'd noticed at a crossroads in Yalta. They're travelling in a bright red VW camper van and explain the campsite rules to me:

You pitch your tent wherever you like. You pay later.

![](/images/2009/07/22/3769457940.jpg)

I pitch my tent next to their van and enjoy a swim in the Black Sea. The water is glorious. The sea is more like a big lake, as there are hardly any waves.

When I get back to the tent, I'm invited to dinner. There's mince with a potato and carrot mash. Very tasty!

We chat until late into the night about travelling, look at photos and swap impressions. They're true globetrotters and have done some extreme trips in the van.

Later the Milky Way wraps us in a sparkling web of stars and we gaze silently up at the night sky for a long time.

Round-bellied and full, we go to bed.
