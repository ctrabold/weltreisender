## Friday, 17 July 2009

## A break in Odessa

![](/images/2009/07/17/3730292714.jpg)

I'm treating myself to another day in Odessa.

Feasting at the breakfast table does me a world of good!

At the next table sits a couple from Norway who came here by train via Poland and are travelling on to Moldova by bus today. They don't speak a word of Russian, but so far they haven't had any problems at all and they love Ukraine. New courage takes root!

Fortified, I lie down for a quick nap after breakfast. When I wake up it's almost 6 pm! The last few days really were exhausting.

At reception I sort out how to get on to the Crimea. There apparently isn't a ferry connection, so once again the choice falls on the train.

This time I get the young woman to write down the key phrases for buying a ticket on a piece of paper. Even so, I'm curious to see how it goes this time.

On my stroll through town afterwards I discover - as expected - a completely different Odessa from yesterday. It's wonderfully warm again, and very mild thanks to the nearby sea. Lots of beautiful and quirky buildings and large parks make wandering around a pleasure.

Sadly, the stress of cycling gets in the way of a lot of beautiful impressions like these - but at the same time it shows you completely different sides of a city. It's OK to know both sides, and now I feel I can leave the city again.

Tomorrow I'll try to get hold of a ticket for the night train to Simferopol.
