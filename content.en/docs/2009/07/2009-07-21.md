## Tuesday, 21 July 2009

## A spaced-out geek and beer for breakfast

![](/images/2009/07/22/3769434298.jpg)

I put off leaving Yalta for as long as I can, stroll around the market and get given a Ukrainian flag there, which rides along with me from now on.

For the first time I'm _a wee bit_ scared of carrying on, because monster climbs and a busy M14 are waiting for me. On top of that, yesterday's ride really took it out of me.

I top up my water supplies and set off. The first climb is the worst. After that things go much more smoothly. The road has a hard shoulder almost all the way and the traffic is lighter than I feared. At the first petrol station I treat myself to a 2-litre bottle of Vanilla Coke, which keeps me fuelled.

And as a reward there are fantastic views of the mountains and the sea.

![](/images/2009/07/21/3769424116.jpg)

In Alushta I stumble upon a campsite right by the sea.

Sadly, the lovely view of the wide blue only lasts a short while, because when I come back from a swim, a Ukrainian family has pitched their tent right in front of my entrance.

![](/images/2009/07/21/3769424082.jpg)

Shortly afterwards we get chatting. He speaks a bit of English and comes across as quite likeable.

His likeability plummets, however, when I'm woken in the middle of the night by his cigarette smoke and drunken slurring: "Hello my german friend, are you awake?". At first I pretend to be asleep, then I try to explain to him that I badly need my night's rest.

After a lot of back and forth he staggers off.

He comes back three more times and I learn all sorts about his life. He's a software developer in Chernobyl and knows SQL and Delphi.

He tells me all of it again the next morning. This time, though, I'm standing on the campsite with a beer in my hand.

As I'm about to get on the bike, another Ukrainian intercepts me, presses a bottle into my hand without asking and raises a toast to me.

An hour later than planned I leave the campsite in high spirits, with lots of waving. New additions: a bracelet that's supposed to bring me luck and a strong hand, plus the address of a bike workshop in Volgograd, just in case...
