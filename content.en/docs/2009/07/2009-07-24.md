## Friday, 24 July 2009

## Farewell 2.0?

![](/images/2009/07/24/3768722623.jpg)

It's only a few kilometres to the Russian border. In Kerch you have to take a ferry across to the mainland.

Yesterday the Danes and I took a rest day and recharged our batteries.

This morning we then hatched the following plan: I enjoy my breakfast ride through the mountains, and at some point they catch up with me. Then we load Rondolf into the VW van and drive over the mountains together to Ленiне (Lenine), where our paths part again.

A wise decision, because the road gets pretty bad and extremely steep. The drive is more like a rollercoaster ride; we're often thrown out of our seats.

By bike this stretch would certainly have taken me many days, which I'd then have been short of in Russia (the visa has already been running since yesterday, 23 July, and is only valid for 30 days).

I ride the remaining kilometres to Керч (Kerch) on the wide and relatively quiet M17 (with a hard shoulder almost all the way). It's dead straight. After all the impressions of the last few days, agonising boredom sets in. And the heat is really taking its toll.

Tomorrow I dare to take another big step east: Russia!

People talk of five to six hours waiting at the border. I hope I'll get straight through this time.

The "internet density" is getting noticeably thinner too. Internet cafés are hardly around any more - and I'd sooo love to show you the pictures from the Crimea!!

With a БАЛТИКА (Baltika beer) I raise a toast to the west and hope I'll be in touch again soon.

До свидания!
