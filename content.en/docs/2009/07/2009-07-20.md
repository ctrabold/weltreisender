## Monday, 20 July 2009

## Yalta - at my age

![](/images/2009/07/20/3769364308.jpg)

First of all, THANK YOU SO MUCH for all your lovely birthday wishes!

The extra year on paper passes me by without a trace. I feel fresh as a daisy and young - because today I'm heading for the sea!

To prove it, I choose the route over the mountains. A bull's-eye, as becomes clear within the first few metres:

Good road surface, and lorries and buses are banned!

The scenery along the route knocks me for six too. I'm constantly taking photos, which also gives me the odd break on this demanding mountain profile.

After 98 kilometres and 5 hours 55 minutes of winding through hairpin bends, I arrive in Yalta overflowing with impressions.

The changes in the surroundings are indescribably moving. There are often moments of absolute peace in the shady forest.

A strong tailwind pushes me across the wide plateau. Almost too fast to enjoy the landscape. Then the view from the plateau straight out over the open sea! Pure goosebumps!

The descent down to sea level is second to none, too. For over 25 kilometres it's a breakneck ride downhill. Surrounded by fragrant conifers and a fantastic view of the sea.

Sending you sunshine from the beach!
