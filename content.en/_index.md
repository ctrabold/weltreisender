## Why this book

This book is about me. It's for you.

There are hundreds of books about cycle touring, travelling around the world and other crazy things. Read them all and let them inspire you.

When I tell people about my journey, many of them think I'm somehow special and that I've done something special that nobody can ever take away from me, and so on.

I don't really get on with words like 'special' or this idea of 'taking away'. All I can tell you is that at some point I could picture what I'm doing, and then I did it. Because somewhere - deep down - I'd always wanted to.

The hardest part for me was accepting the decision itself and letting go of old thoughts and beliefs.

With this book I want to give you strength, so that you make the most of the fantastic opportunity you may have been given.

With this book I want to speak to you and give you the courage to believe in what may still seem impossible to you right now.

To give you clarity: to ignore the others who talk your ear off with their well-meant advice, and to listen to your own inner wish.

And if all that sounds too esoteric for you, then this book is also a nice way to come along on the journey with me.

Because it really was a laugh, and far more entertaining than I'd expected.
